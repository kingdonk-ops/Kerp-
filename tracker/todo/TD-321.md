The access_requests.requested in-app notification (T-0050) is a plain normal-priority entry. Pinning it with pin_key access_requests.decide:<id> and clearing it on approve/reject/withdraw (notifications.clear_pins, project-bound session) would keep inboxes tidy. System admins are not members, so project-scoped notifications cannot reach them; they see the queue through GET /access-requests/pending only.
