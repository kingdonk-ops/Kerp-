oe_settings_deployment uses the standard RLS policy: every session of the tenant reads it, any tenant-level session may write it; the router restricts PATCH to system_admin. A dedicated policy kind (read: tenant; write: app.is_system_admin) in app/core/tenancy/rls.py plus the ORM-scope mirror would make the DB enforce it too. Out of T-0044 scope (core tenancy change).
