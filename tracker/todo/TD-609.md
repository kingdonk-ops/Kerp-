Diff §4.7 'Editor details' wants a mail-health banner for: mail not configured, sender not verified, bounce wiring missing, 'from the same checks as the health probes', never showing credentials. T-0229 shows what exists: GET /settings/deployment-config gives mail_transport (log = nothing is delivered) and mail_from_address (unset = no sender); the banner lists those two problems and never shows the address. GET /admin/system/health (T-0219) has no mail probe (postgresql, redis, object_store, malware_scanner only), and nothing exposes whether the sender identity is verified in SES or whether KERP_MAIL_SNS_TOPIC_ARN is set and the SNS subscription confirmed / a notification was received recently. Backend part: a status-only mail probe (or a mail-health read for System admins) with codes such as sender_unverified and bounce_wiring_missing (no host, ARN, address or credential in the answer); then the banner adds those two lines (frontend/src/admin/comms.ts mailProblems).
