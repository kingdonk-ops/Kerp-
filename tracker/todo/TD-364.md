`backend/app/core/auth/data/common_passwords.txt` (T-0077) holds about 600 generic, publicly known weak passwords plus pattern rules (repetition, keyboard and counting runs, context words). NIST SP 800-63B expects a list of commonly used and compromised passwords; a licence-checked list of the order of 10k to 100k entries (length >= 8 only) would catch more. The loader and the checks need no change; mind the image size and load time (currently a frozenset loaded once). The optional breach lookup (`KERP_PASSWORD_BREACH_CHECK`) covers this where outbound calls are allowed.
