Spec: depth / allowed_parent_keys say 'where the level may sit; allows optional or skipped levels'; a key is never re-keyed once used; pack edits are 'additive'. T-0101 chose: (1) depth 0 <=> allowed_parent_keys empty (a root level); every named parent exists and has a strictly smaller depth, so a level cannot nest under itself (a recursive System/Subsystem or Line/sub-line level needs one level def per tier) and a level cannot be both a root and a child. (2) Once any node (live or deleted) uses a level: key, depth, code_uniqueness and the work_anchor role are frozen and allowed_parent_keys may only gain keys (409); labels, other roles, code_pattern (new codes only) and display_code_template (stored display_codes recomputed) may change. (3) A key that another level names in allowed_parent_keys cannot change even while unused. (4) roles are validated by the API/service only (no portable DB CHECK on a JSON array). Confirm or loosen before the M1 freeze (T-0131); loosening is additive.
