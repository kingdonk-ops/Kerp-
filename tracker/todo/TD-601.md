T-0228's notifications.broadcast e-mail carries a visible opt-out link <KERP_WEB_ORIGIN>/unsubscribe#<token> (token only in the fragment) next to the RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe header. The SPA has no such route yet. Needed: a public page that reads the token from the fragment once, clears it from the URL, shows a confirm button (never unsubscribes on load: link scanners prefetch) and POSTs {"token": "<token>"} to /api/v1/notifications/broadcasts/unsubscribe (body, never the URL; already in the generated client); the answer is always 200 {status: ok}, so the page shows one neutral confirmation. All strings through t(). Until it exists, the mail client's one-click button (List-Unsubscribe-Post) still works.
