`audit.append` takes the impersonator only from `current_impersonator_id`, which
`app.core.auth.deps.get_principal` publishes (like `current_device_id`). Today every
authenticated route depends on `CurrentPrincipal`, so impersonated writes are always v3. A
future route that authenticates some other way (e.g. `require_claims` only) would write its
ledger entries without the admin. T-0278's central guardrail enumerates `app.routes` anyway;
its contract test should also assert that every `allow`-classified mutating route resolves
`get_principal`.
