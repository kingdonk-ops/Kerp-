`audit.append` takes the impersonator only from `current_impersonator_id`, which
`app.core.auth.deps.get_principal` publishes (like `current_device_id`). Today every
authenticated route depends on `CurrentPrincipal`, so impersonated writes are always v3. A
future route that authenticates some other way (e.g. `require_claims` only) would write its
ledger entries without the admin. T-0278's central guardrail enumerates `app.routes` anyway;
its contract test should also assert that every `allow`-classified mutating route resolves
`get_principal`.

2026-11-22 (implementer, T-0279 review round 1): the impersonator travels only in the request's
context var, so ledger entries written by **worker jobs** (SAQ) enqueued during an
impersonation do not name the admin: the worker has no request context and writes v2 entries
attributed to whatever actor the job payload carries. If a job kind that writes ledger entries
becomes reachable under impersonation (`allow`-classified), either carry the impersonator in
the job row (set server-side at enqueue, never from the client) and publish it in the worker, or
classify the enqueuing route `forbid` while impersonating.
