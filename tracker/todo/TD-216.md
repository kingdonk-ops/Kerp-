From PR #51 review round 3 and the user's co-sign redesign (2026-11-13). Mitigations in place: no client PIN hash or other client secret is ever sent to an inspector device (data minimisation; the earlier offline PIN verifier was removed because the inspector could brute-force a 6-digit PIN offline); provisional wet signatures block approval when the template requires a client signature and are badged on the review screen and PDF; client device keys are non-extractable and revoked with the device. Residual: (1) a wet signature can be fabricated by the inspector until the client confirms or rejects it; (2) assertions signed with a stolen key before revocation stay valid; (3) a client who never confirms leaves the record provisional (reminders at 24 h / 72 h, no expiry). Review after Pilot-0.
