RECORD_SOURCES in app/core/db.py is {ui, api, import, sync, ai, system} and is enforced by the BaseRecordMixin flush guard and the audit service. The paste asks for source='automation' (post-submit actions, T-0180) and source='public_link' (public submissions, T-0253). Planner default (not blocking for automation): T-0180 adds 'automation' additively with a short implementer ADR (vocabulary only, no column or hash-format change; the ledger hash covers the source string so old entries are unaffected). 'public_link' is part of ADR 0013 and waits for TD-410. Neither value may be treated like 'ai' (no human confirmation needed), but 'automation' writes may only restrict (out_of_service, quarantined, last_inspected_*), never release (quality-and-inspection.md §Post-submit automation).
