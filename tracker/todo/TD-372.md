---
id: TD-372
title: Invite preview/accept refusals are audited per attempt without a per-user throttle (ledger growth by a signed-in user)
kind: risk
status: open
tasks: [T-0052]
source: implementer
raised: 2026-11-18
---

Every refused preview/accept (and every sign-in carrying a bad invite_token) appends an audit entry: on project-invites:<project> for a known invite, on the tenant chain project-invites for a token that matches no invite. Guessing a 256-bit token is not feasible, but a signed-in user can grow the ledger by repeating bad tokens. Add a per-user progressive lockout (like admin LOOKUP_POLICY) on invite refusals; per-actor limits are also open in TD-138.
