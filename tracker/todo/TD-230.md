register-views.md 3 lists visibility private/team/project/portal and acceptance 5.4 says 'shared view visible to the team', but the oe_saved_view shape has no field saying which team. Planner default (applied to T-0013's contract and T-0020): a nullable team_id (project team from the teams module), required when visibility = team and null otherwise (422); a team view is readable by its owner, the members of that team (teams.service.user_team_ids) and the project's PER-09 (who may edit shared views per 3); a view whose team is deleted falls back to private for the owner. Confirm, or say whether one view can be shared to several teams.

- 2026-11-13 (coordinator): T-0020 was built in parallel and raised the same gap as TD-244 (same default: nullable team_id, required iff visibility = team). Wiring saved_views team visibility to teams.service after T-0013 lands is TD-242; T-0020 keeps depends_on [T-0012].

- 2026-11-13: Decision (user, 2026-11-13): several teams per view, each linked to the view's project; visible only on that project. (planner) Applied: register-views 3 team visibility bullet; T-0074 (view-team link table, migrate T-0020's single team_id) created.
