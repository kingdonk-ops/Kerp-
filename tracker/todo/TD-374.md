Creating an invite creates the account (no credentials) at once; if the invite is revoked or expires unaccepted, the account stays (inactive in practice: it cannot sign in until T-0059/T-0077 give it a password, or by SSO for company members). Also, status expired is stored lazily (on the next accept attempt or re-send); the list derives it from expires_at. Consider a scheduled job that marks expired invites and reports or deactivates accounts that never accepted any invite.
