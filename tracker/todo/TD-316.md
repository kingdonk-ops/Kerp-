run_checks voids a stale override on the side-write pool and appends validation_override_voided to the record's chain <record_type>:<record_id> (pg_advisory_xact_lock). A caller that appended to that chain earlier in its transaction (T-0119 sign-off ledger entry, then run_checks in inspection.submit / inspection.approve) holds the lock: the void waits out lock_timeout, is rolled back and logged, and is retried at the next evaluation. Findings and the block stand (fixed in T-0035 review round 1), but until a void lands an evaluation with the original values would apply the override again, and the void ledger entry is late. The first record-chain caller (T-0119 / inspection submit and approve) must call run_checks before appending to the record chain, or this needs an after-commit job for the void.
