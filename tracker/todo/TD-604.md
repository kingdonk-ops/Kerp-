---
id: TD-604
title: Broadcast template override can drop {message}; broadcast rate limit is not serialised
kind: risk
status: open
tasks: [T-0228]
source: implementer
raised: 2026-11-22
---

(1) notifications.broadcast is an ordinary overridable template (T-0227): a System admin override of its intro without the {message} placeholder would send every later broadcast without its body. Options: a per-template 'must keep placeholder' rule in override_problems, or exclude the template from overrides. (2) The 5-per-24h limit per sender is counted before insert without a lock, so two concurrent POSTs by the same sender at the limit could both pass (at most one extra per race). A per-sender advisory lock (as create_single_run_job) would close it if it matters.
