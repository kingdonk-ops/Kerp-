id: TD-614
title: "Work packs from P6 activities: share an activity's hours (and quantities) over the days that fall inside the work plan's window instead of carrying its full P6 hours"
kind: later
status: open
tasks: [E-04-12]
source: planner
raised: 2026-11-23
---

Seen on the real schedule (2026-11-23): look-ahead packs built from long P6 activities carried the activity's whole budget (one pack showed 27,000+ h for a three-week window). Share hours by the activity's working days inside the plan window (on the activity's P6 calendar once TD-613 interprets calendars; calendar days until then), show the full P6 figure beside it, and never change the P6 mirror. Applies to the planner work plans of ADR 0006 / E-04-12 and to any pack built from programme activities.
