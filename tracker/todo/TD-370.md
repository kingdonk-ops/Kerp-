Diff §4.2 makes eligible whoever carries a valid pending invite for their e-mail that names PER-14; T-0052's criterion says a magic-link request whose invite_token is such an invite. The T-0057 extension point passes the provider only the candidate (tenant, user, e-mail), not the token, so T-0052 implements the §4.2 reading: any valid pending PER-14 invite for the account's e-mail makes it eligible, with or without invite_token in the request (without it the user signs in and lands in the lobby until they open the invite link). Redeem re-checks the same rule. Confirm, or extend the provider signature with the invite reference.
