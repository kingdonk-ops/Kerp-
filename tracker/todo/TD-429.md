The paste (T-0188) has batch auto-generation create kind = itp instances in status pending_qa_review, then Approve & Activate moves them to PENDING. The record lifecycle (quality-and-inspection.md §Unified inspection record, master spec §4.2, T-0111) is PENDING → INSPECTION_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED → INSPECTED_PASSED | INSPECTED_FAILED → … → LOCKED_FOR_TURNOVER; there is no pre-PENDING state, and adding one touches every consumer (field sync, dashboards, run_checks contexts) before the M1 freeze (T-0131). Planner default (applied, not blocking): pending_qa_review is a status of the qms grouping row oe_qms_itp_instance (TD-428), not of the inspection record. Auto-generation writes proposals (instance rows in pending_qa_review with the chosen template revision, the matching rule and no line records); Approve & Activate by PER-06 creates the kind = itp line records in PENDING in one transaction and ledgers the activation (T-0006). Nothing reaches the field before that. Mapping of the paste's other names: PENDING = PENDING; INSPECTED_PASSED and LOCKED_FOR_TURNOVER = as in the spec; 'signed/completed' = any line with a sign-off ledger entry (T-0119) or in SUBMITTED, INSPECTED_PASSED, INSPECTED_FAILED, RE_INSPECT, LOCKED_FOR_TURNOVER, or with an open inspection_request. Propagation: the spec says records pin template_revision_id and new records use the latest revision. Planner default: a master revision publish never re-pins anything by itself; the propagation wizard (PER-06, explicit) re-pins only lines that are PENDING with no request, no answers and no sign-off, adds new lines, and retires removed lines only while PENDING; every other line keeps its pin (T-0119 immutability is absolute). Options for the user: (a) planner default; (b) a new lifecycle state PENDING_QA_REVIEW on the record (needs an ADR and the M1 freeze).
