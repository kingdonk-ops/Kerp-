Legacy concrete ITRs are grids where every row has a Yes/No/N-A combo plus a contractor and a client signature cell (dozens of signature cells per form) under an 'H = Hold, R = Review, M = Monitor' legend. The spec already gates hold/witness/review points through run_checks (quality-and-inspection, construction_control); the gap is the point type (H/W/R/M) on each ITP step, the signer role(s) required per point, and the sign-off record per point, so sign-offs are queryable rather than buried in cells.
