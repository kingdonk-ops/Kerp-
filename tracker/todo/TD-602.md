T-0228 refuses POST /api/v1/notifications/broadcasts locally with 403 IMPERSONATION_ACTION_FORBIDDEN while the caller impersonates (a broadcast e-mails many people in the target's name; it is sent by the real person only). T-0278's central classification should mark it forbid and drop the local check; POST /broadcasts/preview (a count only) and the GET reads can be allow; POST /broadcasts/unsubscribe is unauthenticated (token only) and must stay reachable.
