`offersPortfolio` recognises a `system_admin` only by an `access: system_admin` row. An admin who is a member of every project and holds no portfolio persona gets no All-Projects option. Fix: a `system_admin` flag on `/auth/me` or `/me/projects`. Default: accept (rare; the admin still opens every project one by one).
