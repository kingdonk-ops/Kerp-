Product intake 2026-11-22 (3), item 8. Spec written as plain evidence only (quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation, bullet "Paper-signed pages"; PR #186 review round 1, option (b)):

- A scan of a page signed on paper can be attached to a **specific ITR revision** (inspection record and version) as a file of kind `wet_signed_scan`, with uploader, upload time, the signer's name and party as stated by the uploader, and an optional note (audited).
- It carries no attestation, counts as no sign-off and never replaces any required signature. Internal formal sign-offs keep their attestation; the client co-sign keeps the modes of §Client co-sign with the client's own PER-15 account (user decision R1a, TD-219). The `verification_mode` enum is unchanged.
- The inspection PDF (T-0158) and the dossier (E-05-05) list it as "Wet-signed on paper; scan uploaded by <name> on <date>", next to and never instead of an attestation or co-sign block.

**Open question for the user:** May a scanned paper signature ever count as the client (PER-15) co-sign? If yes, it would follow mode iii: the uploader names the client's PER-15 account, and the co-sign stays provisional (approval blocked as for `CLIENT_SIGNATURE_PROVISIONAL`) until that client confirms in their own account; a rejection returns the inspection as in mode iii. A project rule could then require e-signature instead. Planner default until decided: no, a scan is evidence only.
