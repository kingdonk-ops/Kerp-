Product intake 2026-11-22 (3), item 8 (planner default; user to confirm). Spec written: quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation, bullet "Paper-signed pages".

- A scan of a page signed on paper can be attached to a **specific ITR revision** (inspection record and version) as a file of kind `wet_signed_scan`, with uploader, upload time and an optional note.
- It is evidence, not an e-signature: it carries no attestation, and an internal formal sign-off always needs its own attestation (consistent with §Signing attestation: authority is checked at the action). The signer's name and party are typed in by the uploader and shown as stated.
- Where the project allows it, the scan can stand for a **client or third-party signature** on that ITR revision (client co-sign), recorded as `verification_mode = paper_scan` and reviewed like a provisional wet signature (§Client co-sign).
- The dossier (E-05-05) and the inspection PDF (T-0158) show it as "Wet-signed on paper; scan uploaded by <name> on <date>", next to (never instead of) the attestation block.
- Project setting `inspection.require_e_signature` (default off): when on, the co-sign rule set refuses a paper scan as the client signature and an electronic co-sign mode is needed; the scan can still be attached as plain evidence.

Questions for the user: is a paper scan acceptable as the client's signature at all by default (planner default: yes, reviewed like a provisional wet signature), and should a scan also be allowed for internal sign-offs made on paper during an outage (planner default: no, they are re-signed electronically)?
