T-0053 (from T-0078) wants the 'Create your password' screen to show the invited e-mail read-only and a live length hint from the server policy. POST /api/v1/admin/invites/setup-preview (T-0059) returns only can_set_password, project_code and project_name; the policy's min/max length are known only from a 422 refusal (TD-365 covers adding them to /auth/config or setup-preview). The screen therefore shows the project, a live character count, and the minimum once a refusal states it. Proposed: setup-preview adds the invited e-mail (safe: the caller holds both e-mailed secrets) and min_length/max_length; the frontend then shows them (small follow-up in frontend/src/invite/InvitePage.tsx and frontend/src/password/NewPasswordForm.tsx).
