Product intake 2026-11-21, item 15. RFI slots can be scheduled on public holidays and site shutdown days; nothing warns.

Later: a project working calendar (holidays, shutdown windows; E-04-15 calendar or the E-06-03 working calendar) and a project policy for scheduling a slot on a non-working day: `block` / `allow with confirmation` / `allow`, expressed as a rule in `inspection_request.issue` and `inspection_request.reschedule` (warning or blocking, ADR 0004), not as a hard-wired check. A separate internal warn-or-block option covers days the contractor itself does not work. Depends on where the working calendar lives.
