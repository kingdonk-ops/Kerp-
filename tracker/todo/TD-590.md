Product intake 2026-11-22 (3), item 6 (planner default; user to confirm). Spec-gap across materials-and-traceability, quality and master spec MOD-10 (reinstatement), to be specified when E-06b-01 (asset_integrity: test packs, reinstatement) is broken down.

Planner defaults:
1. **A bolted joint is an Item** (ADR 0003: Items under the work anchor) of a configured item type (for example `bolted_joint`), linked to a test pack (E-06b-01). No new anchor kind.
2. **Breakout event.** A recorded breakout or disassembly event (shutdown, turnaround, maintenance break; who, when, reason, optional work-plan or work-order reference) on one or more joints **resets the joint's integrity status** (for example `made_up → broken_out`) and reopens the related ITP lines (torque / tensioning, flange make-up, leak test) and test-pack checks. The reopening is configuration: the event is a fact (`joint.broken_out_since_last_signoff`) that `run_checks` rule sets (`itp.*`, `test_pack.release`, `mdr.compile`) read; nothing hard-wires a status change on other records.
3. **History kept.** Earlier sign-offs and test results stay as evidence and are marked **superseded** by the breakout (ledger append, never edited or deleted); the dossier shows the current make-up cycle and lists superseded cycles as history.
4. Reinstatement walkdowns (master spec MOD-10) close the cycle with a new make-up sign-off.

Questions for the user: is the breakout event recorded per joint only, or also per test-pack boundary (all joints in it)? Which ITP lines reopen by default?
