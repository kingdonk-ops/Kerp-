memberships.revoke_project (project delete, T-0012/TD-184) now raises TeamGrantsPresentError (DELETE /api/v1/projects/{id} -> 409 team_links_present) while live team_link rows remain on the project. Reasons: a PER-09 session may not write derived rows (RLS, ADR 0008), derived rows must never outlive their link, and revoking them behind the teams module's back would leave live links that reconcile (T-0075) would re-sync onto a deleted project. T-0013 should decide whether project deletion stays blocked until a System admin unlinks the teams, or whether the teams module unlinks (and revokes) on a project-deleted hook.
