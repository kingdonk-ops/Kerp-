T-0277: an impersonation token is never bound to a device (no did, Principal.device_id is None, Principal.is_impersonation is True). PIN unlock already refuses it (no refresh token). When the sync download endpoint (T-0147) or any other device-bound field endpoint is built, it must refuse impersonation principals (403 IMPERSONATION_ACTION_FORBIDDEN or by requiring a device-bound session), with a test.
