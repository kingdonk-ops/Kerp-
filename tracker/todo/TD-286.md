POST /api/v1/devices/{device_id}/wipe-confirmed takes no bearer because every session of a revoked device is dead (diff 4.3: the client wipes, then confirms). The device_id is a client-generated random UUID that only the device, its user and admins see, the call only works on revoked devices, sets wipe_confirmed_at once and is audited; it cannot un-revoke or change anything else. Risk: a leaked id lets someone mark a wipe that did not happen, so admins may trust a false confirmation. Mitigations if needed: a per-device wipe secret issued at enrolment (stored hashed, presented with the confirmation) or device-bound attestation (TD-204).

- 2026-11-15: (planner) Product owner, 2026-11-15: risk accepted for development only; must be fixed before pilot go-live. Covered by T-0080 (per-device wipe secret, credentials-first login device check, uniform answers; phase 0, order 10; diff §4.3). Stays open until T-0080 merges, then resolve with its PR.

- 2026-11-15: (implementer) T-0080: enrolment issues a 256-bit wipe secret once (201 only), stored as wipe_secret_hash (migration 0018); POST /devices/{id}/wipe-confirmed records the wipe only for a revoked device with the right secret, everything else gets one uniform 404 after comparable work; confirmations and refusals audited without the secret. PR: #94.
