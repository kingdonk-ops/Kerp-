verify_chain detects edits, gaps and reordering, but someone with superuser access can delete the newest entries or rewrite a chain and recompute every hash. Periodically record head hashes outside the DB (RFC 3161 timestamp, pyHanko turnover signing per diff §4, or an external log) and verify against them.
