verify_chain detects edits, gaps and reordering, but the table owner (who can DISABLE TRIGGER, re-grant UPDATE/DELETE, turn off FORCE RLS or drop the table) or a superuser can delete the newest entries or rewrite a chain and recompute every hash. Periodically record head hashes outside the DB (RFC 3161 timestamp, pyHanko turnover signing per diff §4, or an external log) and verify against them.
