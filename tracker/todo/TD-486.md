TD-045 RFI spec (quality-and-inspection.md §inspection_requests 5). ADR 0004 Amendment A (accepted 2026-11-10) lets a QA/QC approver override any blocking check on one record. The RFI spec's WITNESS_REQUIREMENT check on inspection.submit blocks an ITR whose ITP line has an external H or W point until the call-out is witnessed, waived or (W only) no-show after valid notice.

Planner default (applied, not blocking): the check is two locked system-default rules, WITNESS_REQUIREMENT_HOLD (H points) with rule param overridable: false, and WITNESS_REQUIREMENT_WITNESS (W points), overridable as usual. validation.service refuses an override request for a finding whose rule has overridable: false with 422 OVERRIDE_NOT_ALLOWED (additive: a rule param read by the T-0035 request path, no change to the frozen oe_validation_override shape). This is an exception to Amendment A for client hold points: an internal approver cannot step over a client's hold.

Options: (a) default: H not overridable, W overridable; (b) both overridable per Amendment A (QA lead approves, audited); (c) neither overridable. On (a) or (c) the planner adds a dated note to ADR 0004 Amendment A recording the exception.
