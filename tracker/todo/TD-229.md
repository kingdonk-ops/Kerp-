TD-073 puts day/night and shutdown teams (with from/to dates) in a roster under staff/project settings, and TD-083 gives the resource pool (crews, rosters, people without a login) to the planner module (planner-and-work-plans 4). handover.md 2 and 8 say 'whole project team' for visibility = project. Planner default applied to T-0013: the teams module holds named groups of KERP users (login accounts with a project membership) used for visibility, sharing and the my_teams filter; it does not hold rosters, shifts, crews or people without a login, which stay in the planner resource pool; 'project team' in handover/planner means every user with a membership on the project, not a teams-module team. A roster team may later reference a teams-module team, never the other way round. Confirm or merge the concepts before E-04-12 / T-0118 are built.

- 2026-11-13: Decision (user, 2026-11-13): confirmed with clarification - teams are for access and permissions, not crews/rosters (TD-073/TD-083 stay with the planner resource pool); 'project team' means everyone on the project. (planner) Applied: diff 4.1 Teams bullet, T-0013 scope.
