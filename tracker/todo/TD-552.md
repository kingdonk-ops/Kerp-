Product intake 2026-11-21, items 31–32. Check of what exists (2026-11-21): PER-09 may assign **any** persona on its project, including PER-09 and hold-authority personas it does not hold (diff §4.1); a System admin grants tenant grants; admin grants never confer `A`/`S`. No code stops an external account (`is_internal = false`) from being assigned a staff persona such as PER-06 or PER-02; only the internal-commercial boundary (T-0295, not built) hides commercial data from external accounts whatever their persona.

1. **Grant ceiling** (item 31). A literal "you can only grant what you hold" rule would stop PER-09 from staffing its project (it rarely holds PER-06 itself) and contradicts diff §4.1. Planner default: keep §4.1 for project personas and add **tests** (not a new default) for the ceilings that do exist: PER-09 cannot grant tenant grants (`system_admin`, `create_projects`) nor memberships on projects where it is not PER-09; `create_projects` holders cannot grant `create_projects`; a service account can never hold an admin grant (diff §4.9); team links cannot carry PER-09/PER-15. The open question (second approver when PER-09 assigns a hold-authority persona it does not hold) is decision TD-561.
2. **External fence** (item 32). Planner default: an `is_internal = false` account can hold only external personas (PER-15, PER-16, PER-14 for subcontractor field workers, and the external inspection party persona of TD-435); assigning a staff persona (PER-01–PER-13 except PER-14) to it is refused with 422 `EXTERNAL_ACCOUNT_PERSONA`, in the membership service, team-link sync and invite acceptance, enforced in code with tests. If an account becomes internal later the restriction lifts; if it becomes external (domain removed) its staff memberships are suspended and PER-09 is notified. The persona list (is PER-14 allowed for external subcontractors?) is decision TD-561.

- 2026-11-22: (planner, product intake follow-up 2026-11-22) TD-561 resolved by the user (2026-11-22): no second approver for PER-09 assigning a hold-authority persona it does not hold; external subcontractor crews may hold PER-14. The fence's persona list is therefore PER-14, PER-15, PER-16 and the TD-435 external inspection party persona (diff §4.9). What remains open here: the ceiling tests and the code-enforced fence (422 EXTERNAL_ACCOUNT_PERSONA).
