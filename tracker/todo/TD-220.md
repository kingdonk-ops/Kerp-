From PR #51 round-4 review (2026-11-13). The assertion's time evidence comes from the client device, but the push batch carries the tablet's clock session, so T-0177 would apply the tablet's skew to the client's wall time. A stolen client key plus a clock set back could slip under a revocation. Use the tablet's reconstructed time of the QR scan as authoritative for the revocation check; keep the client's time as evidence.
