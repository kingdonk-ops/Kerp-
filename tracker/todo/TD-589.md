Product intake 2026-11-22 (3), item 5 (planner default; user to confirm). Later, with document control (E-05-01) and ingestion (E-05-09): when a PDF is uploaded as a revision of a registered document, the SAQ worker extracts the document number from the title block (text layer first, OCR only when there is no usable text layer; heavy libraries imported lazily) and compares it with the registered number (documents-and-mdr.md §Numbering schemes).
- A mismatch or an unreadable title block raises a **warning** on the revision (shown on the document detail and in the upload result), never a block: the upload and the revision stay valid.
- The extracted value, the method (text layer or OCR) and the time are stored with the revision; the warning can be acknowledged with a note (audited).
- Optional later: also compare the revision code.
