T-0016 ships app/modules/uploads/scan.py: a Scanner Protocol and StubScanner that returns 'clean'. uploads.verify calls get_scanner().scan(storage, staging_key) after the SHA-256 check and before the object is copied to its content key; any verdict other than 'clean' rejects the file (reason 'infected'). Add a real scanner (clamd over a socket, streaming the object), install it with set_scanner at worker start-up, add the service to docker-compose, and decide what happens when the scanner is unavailable (fail closed: keep 'verifying' and retry).
