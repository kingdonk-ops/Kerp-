ADR 0003 Amendment 4 names the three scopes without defining them. T-0101 chose: per_parent = unique among the level's nodes under the same parent (the level's roots among themselves); per_level = unique among the level's nodes in the project; per_project = unique among all nodes of the project of any level, symmetric (a per_project code repeats no node's code and no later node of any level may repeat it). Comparison is exact text (case-sensitive; 101.1 and 101.10 differ); a code with leading/trailing whitespace or control characters is refused (422), never trimmed. Soft-deleted nodes do not hold their code. Same-scope duplicates are also a unique-index violation (uq_oe_asset_node_code_scope); the cross-level half of per_project is enforced by the service under the project tree lock only. Confirm, in particular whether per_parent should compare across sibling levels and whether per_project should be cross-level.
