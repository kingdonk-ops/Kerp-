An empty team's link does not stop `projects.delete_project` (only live team-derived rows do), so
a link can outlive its project. Since PR #123 round 1 such a link confers nothing (every sync
skips it and revokes any rows it holds) and stays removable, but the team detail still lists it
and `project_count` counts it: the reads cannot tell a deleted project from one the caller may
not see. Proposal: a projects "deleted" listener (like `add_created_listener`) that the teams
module registers to soft-delete the project's links (audit `link_remove` on `team:<id>`) in the
delete transaction.
