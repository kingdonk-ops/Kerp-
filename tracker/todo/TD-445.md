PR #145 review round 1. The earlier MDR invariant (master spec 4.4, documents-and-mdr.md) blocked on open Cat A punches; the user's 2026-11-21 flags define register_only/excluded as non-blocking, so an internal-audit Cat A punch (excluded) would have contradicted it. Planner default: mdr_effect alone governs the MDR gate; punch templates that use Cat A (all system-default punch templates) default to mdr_effect=blocks; an open Cat A punch always blocks work_anchor.complete regardless of mdr_effect. Alternative: keep Cat A as an additional MDR clause (then excluded/register_only cannot apply to Cat A punches). Not blocking.
