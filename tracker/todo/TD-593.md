Product intake 2026-11-22 (3), item 9 (planner default; user to confirm). Spec written: quality-and-inspection.md §Schedule readiness conflicts (client-supplied material late) and materials-and-traceability.md §Key logic.

- Reuse the existing `supplied_by` (`kaefer | client`) flag of welding-and-nde.md (TD-091) on materials records: a requisition / material line or stock lot carries `supplied_by`, and client-supplied lines carry an `expected_delivery` date (from the client or the delivery schedule).
- A **warning** readiness conflict (resource kind `client_supplied_material`) fires when a client-supplied item's expected delivery (or no date at all) is later than the planned start of the work pack / work-plan line / P6 activity that installs it. It never blocks; the Look-Ahead board (E-04-02) and the AWP constraint view (E-06b-02) show it as a material constraint warning.
- Gap: the materials epic (E-03-03) and procurement (E-03-02) do not yet define `supplied_by` or `expected_delivery` on material lines; add them when those epics are broken down.
