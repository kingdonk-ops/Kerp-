Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, item 3 (guest / subcontractor witness access). One deployment, one tenant: no cross-tenant guests. External people are accounts with `is_internal = false` and project memberships (diff §4.1). Existing coverage: PER-15 Client Representative (granted subtree, `S` witness/co-sign), PER-04/PER-14 for subcontractor field staff, invites with first-password set, no SSO for externals.

Gaps: (1) a lab or NDT subcontractor uploading certificates/reports and a third-party inspector attending witness points have no persona that fits: PER-15 is the client and carries claim verification; PER-04/PER-14 see diaries, workpacks and punch; (2) nothing stops a PER-09 giving an external account a persona that reads internal commercial data (rates, cost, CBS burn, estimates).

A new persona is a core change to the matrix (edge spec §5.2, TD-151). Options:
- (a) **New PER-17 External Inspection Party**: individual assignment only (no team links, like PER-15), granted subtree; `V` on the subtree, `C` only on certificate submissions (they land unverified in the QA queue, T-0181) and on witness attendance for points that name the party; `S` for witness signatures of those points; never `A`; never internal-commercial data. **Planner recommendation.**
- (b) Reuse PER-15 with a `party_type` (`client | third_party | lab`) on the membership (core membership-table change, ADR).
- (c) No persona: subcontractor staff as PER-04/PER-14; labs submit through an upload inbox without an account.

No safe default for the persona: T-0296 and T-0297 are blocked. The commercial boundary (T-0295: routes and fields classed `internal_commercial` answer 404 / are omitted for `is_internal = false` accounts whatever their persona; client-facing claims stay visible to PER-15) is a restrictive planner default and is not blocked; confirm it here too.
