Diff §4 fixes access tokens at 15 minutes and requires rotating refresh tokens but gives no refresh lifetime. T-0008 default: KERP_REFRESH_TOKEN_DAYS=14 (sliding; each rotation issues a new 14-day token; no absolute session cap). Refresh tokens are returned in the JSON body and sent back in the body of POST /api/v1/auth/refresh. Decide: refresh lifetime, whether an absolute session lifetime applies (e.g. re-login every N days), and whether the SPA (T-0024) should get the refresh token as an httpOnly SameSite cookie instead of in the body.

- 2026-11-13: Decision (user, 2026-11-13): 4c. Strict sessions: refresh-token lifetime 12 hours, users sign in again daily. Planner sets the numbers in the spec (KERP_REFRESH_TOKEN_* settings) and checks the interaction with the offline window (TD-170) and the field PIN (TD-135) so offline field sessions are not cut by the daily re-login.

- 2026-11-13: (planner) Applied: diff §4.2 (sessions; daily re-login vs offline); T-0037. SPA cookie transport: TD-203 (since decided: cookie for the SPA, body for Android).
