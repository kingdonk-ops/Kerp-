PR #55 review. `AuthService.login` builds the per-client subject from the full `request.client.host` (`per_client` in `app/core/auth/service.py`). An IPv6 client can rotate through its /64 and get a fresh per-client lock on every address, leaving only the per-account backstop (TD-225). Key IPv6 clients by their /64 (IPv4 unchanged).
