id: TD-320
title: "Access-request filing has no rate limit: withdraw and re-request (no cooldown) can flood a project's PER-09 holders with notifications"
kind: risk
status: open
tasks: [T-0050]
source: implementer
raised: 2026-11-17
---

POST /api/v1/access-requests is limited only by the one-pending rule and the post-rejection cooldown. A withdrawal has no cooldown (diff §4.1), so withdraw + re-request in a loop sends one in-app notification per PER-09 holder each time. Add a per-user (and per user+project) limit with 429 + Retry-After, like the code-lookup ProgressiveLockout; per-IP limits follow TD-138.
