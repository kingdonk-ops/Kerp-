Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, item 2 (user-approved assessment; spec quality-and-inspection.md §Delegation of authority). Fixed: delegation never confers authority (the delegate must hold the qualified persona and valid credentials at the action), SoD and no self-approval apply to the delegate, sign-offs record "Signed by X on behalf of Y". Planner defaults for the open points, not blocking:

1. **Already-assigned items:** yes. During the window the delegate may act on every open item assigned to the delegator, including items assigned before the delegation started; at start the delegate gets one summary notification of those open items.
2. **Delegator keeps authority:** yes; both may act, the first action wins (conditional update).
3. **Who creates:** the delegator; or, for an absent member, a PER-09 of the project or a `system_admin` (audited, the delegator is notified).
4. **Scope and length:** one project per delegation; kinds `hold_point`, `verification`, `sla_escalation`, `tq_approval` (Phase 5); at most 90 days; start may be in the future; revocable at any time.
5. **No chaining:** a delegate's own delegations are never followed; a user cannot delegate to themself; at most one active delegation per delegator, project and kind.
6. **Persona at creation:** a delegate without the qualified persona gets a warning at creation (saved anyway), the action check is what binds.
7. Persona-routed authority (e.g. any PER-06/PER-07 may approve an inspection) gains nothing from delegation; it only routes items addressed to a named person.
