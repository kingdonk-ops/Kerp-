Measured on the real schedule (2026-11-23): the p6 spec 5.1 regex maps 2,867 of 2,960 three-segment names; the rest are 48 spaced code lists, 27 code+qualifier, 10 unspaced chains, 8 without a code. Planner default in docs/spec/modules/p6-xer-format-and-mapping.md 5.2: a spaced list links each listed code as its own work-anchor node (relationship_type shared, no allocation), never expanded as a range; qualifier words (rule parameter) are stored on the link and resolve to the same node; unspaced chains and no-code names go to the Unmatched-Row Linking Screen. Questions: is '728 - 732' a list of two or a range of five? Do OHP/Access/Props mean separate scopes (separate nodes) or just a note? Is '410-2-3' RSW 410 suffixes 2 and 3?
