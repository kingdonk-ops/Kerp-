T-0112 (in the Pilot-0 gate through T-0130) already has a QA review task on OCR discrepancy. T-0181 adds the unified verification queue oe_qms_verification_item. Planner resolution (not blocking): T-0112 is unchanged in scope and keeps one service hook (e.g. equipment.service.request_cert_review) that today raises its QA review the way T-0112 builds it; T-0194 (outside the gate) redirects that hook to create a verification item (target_kind equipment_calibration) and makes the two-lane window the place where the review is decided. T-0112 must not depend on T-0181. The same applies to the credentials upload (T-0118) and T-0195.
