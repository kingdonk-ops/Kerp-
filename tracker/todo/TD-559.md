Product intake 2026-11-21, items 1–4 and 6 (spec edits in documents-and-mdr.md §Dossier builder, revisions and verification, planner defaults). Open points for the E-05-05 breakdown:

- **Client acceptance record:** how the client's acceptance of a review revision (Rev A/B) is recorded: portal co-sign (E-07-02), an internal record of the client's transmittal reply with the reply attached, or both. The `mdr.compile` default rule for Rev 0 reads it as a fact.
- **Revision scheme:** confirm letters for review rounds and numbers for final issues (Rev A → Rev 0) per project, or make the scheme a project setting.
- **Rev 0 without a review round:** allowed when the client waives review (recorded waiver) or never.
- **B-LTA augmentation:** who runs the archive-timestamp refresh, how often, and that every augmented version keeps its own stored SHA-256.

Question for the user: confirm the planner defaults in the spec and choose the acceptance record mechanism.
