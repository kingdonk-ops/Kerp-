Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, item 6 (spec diff §4.7 *Feature flags*). Planner defaults, not blocking:

1. Only `system_admin` sets flags (tenant value and per-project overrides); PER-09 sees the effective flags of its projects, read-only.
2. Staged rollout = a flag `on` for named pilot projects first (project overrides), then tenant-wide; no percentage or per-user rollout.
3. A flag gates **availability** of a feature (UI entry points and the feature's endpoints, which answer 404 `FEATURE_DISABLED` while off). It never relaxes authorization, `run_checks`, RLS or a Pilot-0 rule, and turning a flag off never deletes data.
4. Flags are code-declared registry entries (category `feature_flags`, type `bool`); an undeclared key is refused.
