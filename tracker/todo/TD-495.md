T-0222 adds no migration. GET /admin/system/audit orders by (created_at, id) and filters on created_by, device_id, a chain_key prefix and payload keys (JSONB containment on PostgreSQL); audit_ledger only has the mixin indexes and the unique (tenant_id, scope_id, chain_key, seq), so on a large ledger the list is a sequential scan plus sort. Add, with an additive migration (frozen DDL): an index on (tenant_id, created_at DESC, id DESC), and when needed a GIN index on payload (jsonb_path_ops) and indexes on created_by / device_id. Verify-all (admin.audit_verify_all) streams the tenant's ledger in one statement (it uses the unique index order) with a 3600 s timeout, reports no progress and cannot resume; nothing stops two admins from queueing it at the same time. Split it per scope or add progress and a single-flight guard once ledgers are large.
