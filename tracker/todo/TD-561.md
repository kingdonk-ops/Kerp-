Product intake 2026-11-21, items 31–32 (detail in TD-552). Two questions for the user:

1. **Second approver for hold-authority personas.** PER-09 may assign any persona on its project (diff §4.1), including hold-authority personas (PER-02, PER-06, PER-07, PER-11) it does not hold itself. Should such an assignment need a second approver (for example a PER-13 or another holder of that persona)? Planner default: **no**. Keep diff §4.1: the assignment is audited, the SoD warning stays where it applies, and authority is still checked at the action (credentials, `run_checks`).
2. **Personas for external accounts.** Planner default: an `is_internal = false` account may hold only PER-15, PER-16, PER-14 and the external inspection party persona (TD-435), and every other staff persona is refused (422 `EXTERNAL_ACCOUNT_PERSONA`). Is **PER-14 (Field Worker) allowed for external subcontractor crews**? Planner recommendation: yes, because subcontractor field workers sign in by magic link (diff §4.2).

Not blocking: TD-552's tests and the fence build against the defaults.
