After the round-1 fix of PR #159, `recheck_upload` refuses a re-check while the file's current
verify job is `queued` or `running` (409 `CHECK_RUNNING`), so the re-check never starts a
second job next to a live one by itself. Two paths can still overlap:

- The queue monitor aborts a `running` verify job (`jobs.service.abort_job` → `failed`,
  `ABORTED`). The SAQ cancel does not stop the `asyncio.to_thread` call to `verify_upload`, so
  that thread may still be checking when an admin re-checks the now "stuck" file.
- After a re-check, the replaced job is still `failed`; a queue-monitor retry of it
  (`retry_failed_job`) runs `verify_upload` again next to the new job (`verify_upload` does
  not compare `file.job_id` with its own job).

Either way both jobs share the quarantine object, and each deletes it before it writes the
outcome: the later writer may reject a clean file as `missing_object`, and the other leaves
an orphan content object. Fail-safe (nothing is published unscanned; the user uploads
again). Possible fix: `verify_upload` takes its job id and stops when `file.job_id` names
another job, or per-job quarantine keys.
