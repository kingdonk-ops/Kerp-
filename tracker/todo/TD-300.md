T-0038 interpretation of diff §4.2 'renewed after it was idle longer than KERP_PIN_IDLE_MINUTES': idle = now - iat of the presented refresh token (sign-in, rotation or unlock). Stateless and fail-closed, but it overestimates real inactivity by up to one access-token lifetime (15 min): a user who stops at minute 14 after a rotation and comes back at minute 31 is asked for the PIN after 17 min of real inactivity. Alternative: track the session's last authenticated request (per-session last-seen in Redis or the device row's last_seen_at). Exactly at the limit counts as idle (>=). Confirm or choose the alternative.

- 2026-11-15: (implementer, PR #95 review) Settings now refuse KERP_PIN_IDLE_MINUTES below KERP_ACCESS_TOKEN_MINUTES: an active client refreshes once per access token, so a shorter idle limit would ask for the PIN on every refresh.
