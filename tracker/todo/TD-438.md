Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, items 4 and 7. ADR 0017 records the user-approved rule that SCIM tokens and API keys bind the tenant from the credential (the deployment tenant, looked up under tenant RLS), not from a JWT, as an exception to CLAUDE.md §6 limited to those two credentials. Planner defaults for the open points, not blocking (Phase 5 except SCIM):

1. **Service account** = an `auth_user` row with a new `account_type` (`human | service`, default `human`; core-table column, ADR 0017). It never signs in interactively (no password, SSO, magic link, PIN, device or invite), is excluded from seats, presence and broadcasts, and holds project memberships like a user.
2. **Who manages keys:** `system_admin` only, for tenant-wide and project-scoped keys. PER-09 may list the keys that touch its projects but not create them.
3. **Scopes:** `read`, `write`; capability = scope ∩ the service account's memberships; `A` and `S` are always removed.
4. **Limits:** expiry required, at most 365 days; 600 requests per minute per key (registry entry `api_key_rate_per_minute`, 60-6000).
