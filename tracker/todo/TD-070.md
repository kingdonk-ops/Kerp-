The legacy Inspector role grants PERFORM_INSPECTION together with APPROVE_INSPECTION, SUPERVISOR_INSPECTION_EDIT, DELETE_INSPECTION and RETIRE_PERFORMED_INSPECTION, so one person can perform and approve the same record. Decide whether KERP forbids self-approval (and soft-delete only) for inspection records.

- 2026-11-10: Decision (user, 2026-11-10): Configurable per project; default forbids self-approval; soft-delete only.
