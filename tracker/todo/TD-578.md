T-0277 exposes Principal.is_impersonation / impersonator_user_id. When T-0243 adds last_login_at, last_active_at, presence and the per-user session index, a request authenticated by an impersonation token must not update the target's (or the admin's) telemetry or presence, and impersonation sessions must not be listed as normal sessions in either user's index (T-0277 AC). Nothing of that exists yet, so T-0277 has nothing to suppress.
