User (T-0184, 2026-11-21): extensions are approved by the QA approver (default PER-06), or by PER-13 Quality Director when PER-06 is the requester; no self-approval. Edge spec §5.2: PER-13 is portfolio 'Audit oversight', QA approval (A) is PER-06 (PER-07 site holds). ADR 0004 Amendment A has a precedent for routed non-PER-06 approval (override requests: PER-02 when the requester is PER-06). Diff §4.1: admin grants never confer quality authority. Planner reading (applied, not blocking; Phase 3 task): the routed PER-13 approval is a narrow capability for this one record type, like Amendment A, held only by a PER-13 with access to the project, never the requester, audited; it does not give PER-13 approval in other queues (T-0181 items, escalations TD-418). Question: confirm, or route to another PER-06 of the project / PER-02 instead.
