id: TD-455
title: "Queue monitor: retry/abort commit the job write before the audit entry (two transactions); an audit-commit failure leaves the action unaudited"
kind: risk
status: open
tasks: [T-0218, T-0221]
source: implementer
raised: 2026-11-21
---

T-0218's `jobs.service.retry_failed_job` / `abort_job` change the job row in a unit of work bound
to the job's own scope (commits first), then append the `admin-system:<tenant>` ledger entry in
the caller's request session (commits when the request ends). If that later commit fails, the
retry or abort stands without its audit entry. Options: after T-0274 (ADR 0016) do both in the
request session (one transaction), or write the audit entry first in its own committed unit of
work and mark it pending/confirmed.
