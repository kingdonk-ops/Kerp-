The spec example scopes the policy to kerp_app_role. KERP's role names are deployment config (CI uses kerp_app), and a policy for one role leaves other non-superuser roles with default-deny anyway. T-0007 creates the policy without a TO clause (PUBLIC), which binds every role that is not SUPERUSER/BYPASSRLS. Confirm, or name the role in the spec.
