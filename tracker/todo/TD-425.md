The paste wants eligibility.is_product_approved(project_id, anchor_id, spec_product_code, selected_product_code, as_of_date) in CEL. CEL functions in KERP are pure and must give identical results on celpy and the JS runtime with shared conformance fixtures (T-0017, T-0110); a function that queries the database breaks parity and offline evaluation. Planner default (applied, not blocking): the eligibility service (server) and the synced bundle (client) resolve context.product_approvals for the record's project, anchor lineage and inspection date (baseline list + approved substitutions in scope, as of that date); the registered pure function is is_product_approved(context.product_approvals, spec_product_code, selected_product_code) -> bool on both runtimes, with parity fixtures. The paste's project_id/anchor_id/as_of_date become inputs of the resolution, not of the function.
