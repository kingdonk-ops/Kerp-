Left open by TD-121 decision 4c (user, 2026-11-13), which settled only the lifetimes (12-hour refresh from sign-in, daily re-login). Default kept: refresh token in the JSON body of login/refresh, stored by the SPA in sessionStorage (T-0008, T-0024); it also suits the Capacitor build, where cookies are awkward. A cookie would reduce XSS exposure of the refresh token but needs CSRF protection on /auth/refresh. Not blocking.
