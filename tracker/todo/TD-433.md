Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, item 1 (user-approved assessment; spec quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation). Planner defaults applied, not blocking; confirm or change:

1. **Formal sign-off kinds** (attestation required): `itp_hold_release`, `itp_witness` (single-row H/W sign-off), `inspection_approve`, `substitution_approve` (internal_qa route), `shelf_life_extension_approve` (Phase 3), `tq_approve` (Phase 5). Not attested: the inspector's submit signature, R/S/M bulk close-out (TD-095) and client co-sign (it has its own verification modes).
2. **Online re-authentication:** the signing PIN (T-0162's `oe_identity_signing_pin`, opened to every user, same per-signer lockout) or the account password (T-0068 lockout). SSO-only users use the signing PIN; setting or resetting it needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes. Entra step-up (`prompt=login`, `max_age`) is not in v1.
3. **Grant:** one single-use signing grant per sign-off, bound to user, session, kind, record id and version; TTL `signing_reauth_ttl_minutes` (registry, default 5, 1-15). No multi-signature "signing session" window.
4. **Offline (Android tablet; sync branch T-0310):** device-PIN re-entry at the moment of signing even while the app is unlocked (local keystore check, counts toward the local lockout); `reauth_method = device_pin_offline`, which needs no server signing grant. At sync: a revoked or foreign device is refused as for any mutation; an `intent_version` current at `occurred_at` is accepted, an older one is kept, effective and flagged `intent_outdated` (never 409; the server renders that version's i18n text, never client-supplied text); outside the offline grace/window it is flagged `grace_exceeded` (kept, effective). **Signer standing:** `signer_standing(user_id, from_ts, to_ts)` in the identity service answers from durable standing events persisted in the identity table `oe_identity_account_standing_event` (`deactivated | reactivated | sessions_revoked | signed_in`, with `occurred_at` and, for `signed_in`, the `device_id`), written by admin deactivation and reactivation (T-0014 path), force sign-out (T-0244), SCIM (T-0299) and every successful sign-in; it never reads the expiring Redis session index. The window starts at the signer's last successful sign-in on the mutation's `device_id` at or before `occurred_at` (no such record: at the signer's earliest recorded event, fail-safe) and ends at `occurred_at`; when `time_confidence` is neither `MONOTONIC_RECONSTRUCTED` nor `VERIFIED_ONLINE`, it ends at the sync time instead (fail-safe against a backdated clock). If a deactivation or a session revocation of the signer falls inside the window, the sign-off is **kept as evidence but not effective**: it does not count as signed, releases no hold and closes no row, and it is flagged `signer_inactive`. A deactivation and reactivation both before the window start do not flag it; a later reactivation never makes a flagged sign-off effective. **QA routing:** a `signer_inactive` sign-off creates a QA verification item of target kind `offline_signoff_signer_inactive`, whose provider is registered in `signing/service.py`. Neither approve nor reject ever makes the original sign-off effective: the provider's `apply_approved` records the flag as reviewed and `apply_rejected` records the entry void, each only as a ledger append; the flagged entry is never altered and nothing is released or closed. The point is resolved only by a fresh attested online sign-off by a qualified signer through the normal consumer path, or by recording the flagged entry void. Nothing is dropped and nothing is released on a deactivated signer's authority.
5. **IP and device:** IP as observed by the server behind trusted proxies only (`FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPS`); for offline sign-offs the sync request's IP with `ip_source = sync`. Stored inside the immutable ledger payload, retained with the record (TD-175 retention). **Privacy/retention position:** the IP and the user-agent family sit in the immutable, hash-chained ledger and cannot be erased or redacted later (an erasure request cannot remove them without breaking the chain). This is accepted for evidentiary sign-offs (legitimate interest / legal obligation to prove who signed), and only the minimum fields are kept: IP, user-agent family (no full user-agent string, no geolocation, no device fingerprint beyond the enrolled device id). To be confirmed with the data-protection owner.
6. **Pilot-0:** sign-offs made before T-0289/T-0291 ship carry no attestation and stay valid; T-0146 is unchanged.
