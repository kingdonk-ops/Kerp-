Enterprise roadmap 2026-11-21, item 1 (user-approved assessment; spec quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation). Planner defaults applied, not blocking; confirm or change:

1. **Formal sign-off kinds** (attestation required): `itp_hold_release`, `itp_witness` (single-row H/W sign-off), `inspection_approve`, `substitution_approve` (internal_qa route), `shelf_life_extension_approve` (Phase 3), `tq_approve` (Phase 5). Not attested: the inspector's submit signature, R/S/M bulk close-out (TD-095) and client co-sign (it has its own verification modes).
2. **Online re-authentication:** the signing PIN (T-0162's `oe_identity_signing_pin`, opened to every user, same per-signer lockout) or the account password (T-0068 lockout). SSO-only users use the signing PIN; setting or resetting it needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes. Entra step-up (`prompt=login`, `max_age`) is not in v1.
3. **Grant:** one single-use signing grant per sign-off, bound to user, session, kind, record id and version; TTL `signing_reauth_ttl_minutes` (registry, default 5, 1-15). No multi-signature "signing session" window.
4. **Offline (Android tablet):** device-PIN re-entry at the moment of signing even while the app is unlocked (local keystore check, counts toward the local lockout); `reauth_method = device_pin_offline`; accepted at sync under the offline-window/grace rules of diff §4.2, flagged (not dropped) when outside them.
5. **IP and device:** IP as observed by the server behind trusted proxies only (`FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPS`); for offline sign-offs the sync request's IP with `ip_source = sync`. Stored inside the immutable ledger payload, retained with the record (TD-175 retention).
6. **Pilot-0:** sign-offs made before T-0289/T-0291 ship carry no attestation and stay valid; T-0146 is unchanged.
