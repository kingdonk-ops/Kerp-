The 2026-11-21 addendum (T-0182) says an escalated verification item grants co-review authority to escalate_to_persona (PER-02 Project Manager or PER-13 Quality Director). Persona matrix (edge spec §5.2): QA authority (A) is PER-06 (and PER-07 for site holds); PER-02 has 'Review only', PER-13 'Audit oversight'. Diff §4.1: admin never confers quality authority; authority comes from a qualified persona and the credentials the action needs (TD-099/TD-100, TD-151). Granting approval by escalation widens who can verify a calibration cert, credential or MTR. Planner default (safe, applied, not blocking): escalation sets status escalated, reassigns/adds the escalation target as a watcher, notifies both, and is audited; approve/reject still needs the project's QA reviewer authority (the configured QA persona, default PER-06). Override requests (ADR 0004 Amendment A) keep their own approver routing (PER-06, or PER-02 when the requester is PER-06). Options: (a) keep the default; (b) allow the escalation target to approve when it also holds a QA-capable persona on the project; (c) allow PER-02/PER-13 to approve escalated items outright (authorization change: needs an ADR and a persona-matrix change).
