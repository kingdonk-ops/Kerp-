From the PR #89 round-1 review (T-0043). device_api.py (enrol, revoke) and deps.py resolve_request_device do not map RevocationUnavailableError to 503, unlike router.py and bearer.py, so a Redis outage answers 500 there (test_devices.py asserts the current 500 for a failed session kill after the revoke commit). Consider a global exception handler for RevocationUnavailableError -> 503 instead of per-endpoint mapping.
