Diff §5 and T-0112 define oe_equipment_asset as the calibrated test-equipment register (TD-061: test equipment is a dedicated module); the plant/fleet register is epic E-03-07 (Phase 3). The 2026-11-21 paste puts fleet vehicles/plant (LV-042 pre-start QR) into oe_equipment_asset in Phase 1. Planner proposal (T-0249): additive columns on oe_equipment_asset: equipment_class (instrument | vehicle | plant, default instrument), requires_calibration (bool, default true for instrument, false otherwise; the calibration gate and equipment.activate cert rule apply only when true), asset_number (fleet number), custodian_user_id (nullable, a project member). E-03-07 then builds on these columns. Options: (a) pull forward as proposed; (b) wait for E-03-07 (pre-starts then anchor to nodes/items only in Phase 1). No safe default: T-0249 blocked until decided.

- 2026-11-21 (planner, product-owner decisions 2026-11-21): The equipment decision (equipment is a link, TD-411 resolved) does not answer this question: whether fleet vehicles and plant enter the Phase-1 equipment register stays open and T-0249 stays blocked. Equipment links (T-0250) work for the existing register entries (instruments) without T-0249 and pick up vehicles and plant once T-0249 lands.
- 2026-11-21 (coordinator, product-owner decision 2026-11-21): Resolved — fleet vehicles and plant join the Phase-1 equipment register (T-0249 unblocked). Compliance (calibration due, monthly inspections) is driven by the register's `requires_calibration` and the calibration register (T-0112); inspections reach the equipment through links (T-0250, T-0288).
