PR #55 review round 1. LOGIN_ACCOUNT_POLICY escalates like the other policies and its 24 h window is not extended, so only the first lock costs 51 failures (easy from 9+ addresses, 6 free per client); afterwards one request per lock expiry keeps every client of that account out (lock up to 15 min). While locked, the owner's retries also advance their own per-client counter. Options for the owner: non-escalating or short backstop delay; let a client with a clean per-client record (or a remembered device cookie) through the backstop; accept until SSO (TD-043) replaces passwords for staff.
