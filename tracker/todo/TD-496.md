Diff §4.7 and T-0219 ask for status ok | degraded | down per probe but do not define degraded. T-0219 implemented: ok = healthy within 1 s (DEGRADED_AFTER_S); degraded = healthy but slower than 1 s, or nothing real to probe (the always-clean stub scanner, a storage backend without a probe); down = failed, raised, timed out (2 s, PROBE_TIMEOUT_S) or not configured (no Redis URL). Constants in backend/app/modules/admin/health.py. Decide whether the threshold should be configurable (runtime settings registry, T-0230) and whether a stub scanner in production should be down instead of degraded (the worker already refuses it, T-0213).
