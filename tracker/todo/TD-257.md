Product owner decision 2026-11-14 (diff §4.2 Offline grace): after the 12 h session ends offline, the PIN keeps unlocking and queueing for offline_grace_hours (default 24) after the session end. The earlier user decision (TD-170, TD-206) let a device edit offline for the whole offline window (default 7, max 14 days). Planner reading applied (not blocking): the effective offline edit limit is the earlier of session end + grace and last contact + window, so with defaults a worker edits offline for at most ~36 h after the last sign-in; offline_window_days stays as an upper bound (grace may be set up to 24 x window days) and the sync/outbox stays sized for 14 days. Confirm, or say whether the 7-day window should instead extend the grace for multi-day offline sites (e.g. remote shutdowns).

- 2026-11-14: (planner) Resolved: product owner confirmed the planner reading. Recorded in diff §4.2 (Offline grace), spec README and DEVELOPMENT-PLAN.
