title: Promotion fails red when CI runs on dev finish out of order (promote.yml checks origin/dev, not the run's head_sha)
kind: bug
status: open
tasks: []
source: planner
raised: 2026-11-21
---

promote.yml is triggered by workflow_run of CI on dev but resolves SHA from origin/dev; when an older run finishes after a newer push, ci-gate has not yet passed on the newer dev commit and the job errors ("ci-gate has not passed on dev@SHA") instead of skipping. Fix: promote the run's head_sha (if it is still an ancestor of dev and main is its ancestor) or exit neutral when dev has moved; test in scripts/tests/test_workflows.py.
