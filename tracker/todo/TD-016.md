Starlette >=1.10 emits StarletteDeprecationWarning when starlette.testclient uses httpx and recommends httpx2. T-0001 keeps httpx (dev extra) and does not filter the warning. Evaluate httpx2 (provenance, licence) and switch the dev extra once vetted; then consider -W error::DeprecationWarning in pytest config.
