T-0043 makes a client device_id belong to the account that enrolled it (unique per tenant; another account enrolling the same id gets 409), and a session to at most one device. A tablet shared by a crew (several workers signing in one after another) therefore needs one device_id per account (per-account installation/profile on the client), and a revoke of one worker's device wipes only if the client keys its store per account. Diff 4.3 does not say whether shared tablets exist in the pilot. Decide: keep one-account-per-device_id (current), or allow a device row per (device_id, user) with a revoke that wipes the whole physical device (and then warns about every account on it).
