Diff §4.2 says reset requests use 'the same per-mailbox cap as magic links, keyed by tenant + e-mail' (TD-224). T-0077 applies the same policy (`MAGIC_LINK_REQUEST_POLICY`: 4 back to back, then 1 min doubling to 15 min over 1 h) on its **own** counter (scope `pwreset`), so a reset flood cannot delay a Field Worker's magic link and vice versa; one address can therefore be sent up to 4 + 4 e-mails back to back. If the cap is meant to be one shared counter for all auth e-mails to a mailbox, change the scope in `PasswordResetService`. Product-owner / planner decision.
