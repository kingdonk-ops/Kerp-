PR #116 review suggestion. backend/app/core/mail/sns.py:206-228 and the ses_events handler in backend/app/core/mail/router.py:116-130 (threadpool call to sns.handle at l.124). Failed fetches are not cached, so each new .pem path on the regional SNS host causes a 5 s outbound GET on a threadpool worker. Options: rate-limit /ses-events, cache failures briefly, or restrict the path to the SimpleNotificationService-*.pem shape.
