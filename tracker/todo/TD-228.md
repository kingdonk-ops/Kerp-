The spec names teams only as a Backbone nav item (diff 6.1), the register filter op my_teams and saved-view visibility team (register-views 2, 3); it says nothing on scope or who manages them. Every place that uses them is project-scoped (saved views are per project; handover/planner rosters live under project settings). Planner default applied to T-0013: teams are project-scoped rows (project RLS), created/renamed/deleted and staffed by holders of the admin capability on the project (PER-09) only; a system_admin gets team rights only by granting itself PER-09 on the project (diff 4.1); every project member can list teams and see their members; team members must hold an active membership on the project. Confirm, or name tenant-level/cross-project teams or team leads who manage their own team.
