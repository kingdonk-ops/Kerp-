Per the TD-013 decision, `scripts/review_gate.py` drops every `- YYYY-MM-DD...` line from tracker files before computing the patch-id, so planner/coordinator notes stay bookkeeping. Side effect: a dated note that changes scope (e.g. "Absorbed T-0066") is invisible to the gate. Options: keep (accepted risk), or only normalise notes appended after the approval (needs the approved head SHA), or require scope decisions to live in Goal/Acceptance criteria. Allocated while `gh pr list` was unavailable - check-ids will catch a clash.
