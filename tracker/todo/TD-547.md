Product intake 2026-11-21, item 11. Some tests are witnessed on site but their result arrives later (adhesion pull-off read in the lab, concrete cylinder breaks, lab chemistry). Today the RFI slot goes to `witnessed` and the linked ITR must then be submitted with a result; there is no state for "witnessed, result pending".

Planner recommendation: put the waiting state on the **ITR, not on the slot**. The slot stays `witnessed` (the attendance fact is complete, quality-and-inspection.md §inspection_requests §4 says the result is never a slot state). The ITR gets a `results_awaited` marker (or a lifecycle state between `INSPECTION_REQUESTED` and submit) with an expected-by date and the pending check keys; submit stays blocked by a `RESULTS_PENDING` check in `inspection.submit` until the result fields are filled; the RFI notification PDF prints "Results awaited" for that slot; an overdue expected-by date notifies the requester and PER-06.

Needs: a decision on marker vs new lifecycle state (the inspection lifecycle is core, T-0111), the PDF label in §10.3, and the late-result entry (who enters it, attachments from the lab, an external lab party per T-0297).
