The pasted 'T-0074' (now T-0226..T-0229) gives system_admin tenant-wide broadcast targeting (all active users, any project, audience category, persona) and PER-09 targeting within its own projects. Diff §4.1 (Manual notifications, TD-237) allows only a project's PER-09 to send notifications through the API.

Planner default applied (not blocking): e-mail broadcasts are a separate channel from in-app notifications; PER-09 targets only its own projects (as TD-237); System admins may broadcast tenant-wide because they already list every user and membership (diff §4.1). Every broadcast is audited; broadcast e-mails carry List-Unsubscribe and a one-click opt-out for broadcasts only.

Question for the user: confirm System-admin tenant-wide broadcasts, and whether broadcasts also create in-app notifications.
