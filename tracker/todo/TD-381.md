T-0074 (migration 0032) copied every oe_saved_view.team_id into oe_saved_view_team and relaxed ck_oe_saved_view_team_matches_visibility to 'team_id IS NULL OR visibility = team'. The column is never read, is cleared on the next write of a view and is NULL on new rows; it stays only because dropping a column is destructive. Once every deployment is past 0032: write an ADR in docs/decisions/, then a migration that drops the column and its CHECK (the downgrade re-adds it nullable and backfills one team per team view from the link table, as 0032's downgrade does), and remove SavedView.team_id from the model.
