The compose worker disables the image's HTTP HEALTHCHECK (it probes the API's /health); add a worker-specific liveness probe (e.g. SAQ worker stats heartbeat in Redis). On SIGTERM the worker gives running jobs an 8 s grace period, then SAQ cancels them and run_job marks them failed ('Job timed out or was cancelled'); a deploy should rather put them back to queued for another worker (and distinguish timeout from shutdown).
