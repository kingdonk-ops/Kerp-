Migration 0002 revokes UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE from PUBLIC and from the migrating role and adds append-only triggers. When migrations and runtime use separate roles (TD-056), the deploy must grant the runtime role SELECT, INSERT only, and the app could refuse to start if app.core.audit.guard.ledger_guard_problems reports anything.
