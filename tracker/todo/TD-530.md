backend/app/core/db.py takes pool_size/max_overflow but the app always uses the defaults; with several API processes and workers the total connection count cannot be tuned against the PostgreSQL limit. Fix: KERP_DB_POOL_SIZE / KERP_DB_MAX_OVERFLOW settings (documented, validated) passed to every engine.
