T-0019 delivers every configured channel synchronously in notify(), after the in-app row is flushed but before the caller commits. A remote channel would then send for a notification that may roll back, and slow the request. When the first remote channel lands (T-0144), enqueue the fan-out as a job after commit (jobs.create_job), persist each channel's result and retry failures. In-app stays synchronous (it is the row).
