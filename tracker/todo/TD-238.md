T-0019 delivers every configured channel synchronously in notify(), after the in-app row is flushed but before the caller commits. A remote channel would then send for a notification that may roll back, and slow the request. When the first remote channel lands (T-0144), enqueue the fan-out as a job after commit (jobs.create_job), persist each channel's result and retry failures. In-app stays synchronous (it is the row).

- 2026-11-22: (planner, product intake 2026-11-21, item 17) The per-channel delivery log here is part of the wider notification rule and delivery-log model in TD-549 (statuses pending/sent/failed/skipped with reasons, Outbox card on ITR and RFI detail).

- 2026-11-22: (planner, product intake 2026-11-22 (3)) TD-549 was resolved by the user decision on opt-out (call-outs are transactional and ignore opt-out); the wider rule and delivery-log model now continues in TD-587.
