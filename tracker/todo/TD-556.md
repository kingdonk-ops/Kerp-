Product intake 2026-11-21, item 39. P6 (T-0104, T-0129) and asset imports (T-0138) already stage and preview before commit. Later: an **undo** of a committed import, allowed only while nothing refers to the nodes it created (no inspection, issue, RFI, checkout, link or child node created since); otherwise the undo is refused, listing what refers to them. Undo is a soft delete of the created rows plus a revert of updated fields from the import's before-values, one audit entry, through the owning service.
