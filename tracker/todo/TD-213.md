DEVELOPMENT-PLAN Pilot-0 says the client signs on the inspector's device with a PIN instead of the portal, but no task covers it and the identity of the client signer is a contractual choice. T-0119 (ledger) and T-0150 (submit signature) cover only the inspector and the reviewer. Options: (a) the client representative has a KERP account (PER-15) on the project and enters their own PIN on the inspector's device, recorded as a second ledger entry; (b) typed name + drawn signature captured as a signature answer, no account; (c) no client signature in Pilot-0. Planner leans (a); no safe default, so no task is cut until decided.
