In the legacy inspection system every register entry is an 'item' (Asset, RSW, Scope, Personnel, Material, Equipment Register, WPS, Welds), item types pick typed attributes from a global attribute catalogue, and RSW is just an item type under Asset. In KERP, RSW is the work anchor and Item is the inspection anchor. Decide whether personnel (VOC/qualifications), materials (bolt/grout batches, certs) and test equipment (calibration expiry) map to dedicated KERP modules (competency, materials, qms calibration) or a generic register on the one-engine item_types, and how templates look them up. Source: legacy exports reviewed 2026-11-10 (itemcat (9) is the latest register).
