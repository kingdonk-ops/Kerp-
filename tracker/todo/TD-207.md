Diff §4.2 specifies offline local unlock against a key in the platform keystore, which the Capacitor Android build has (T-0143). Web-only PWA field apps are also enrolled devices (§4.3) but have no keystore. Specify and build their local unlock, e.g. a non-extractable WebCrypto key in IndexedDB that wraps the data key, with PIN-derived unwrapping and the same lockout numbers, or decide that PWA apps need online sign-in.
