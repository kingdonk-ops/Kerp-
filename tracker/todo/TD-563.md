Product intake 2026-11-22, item 8. Spec edit made (commercial-and-claims.md §Earned value counts verified completion, planner default): EV credits budget only for progress verified by passed ITP lines (`INSPECTED_PASSED` / `LOCKED_FOR_TURNOVER`), self-reported progress shown beside it as "reported", plus a planned vs verified-complete view per area or work package. Master spec MOD-03 defines CPI = EV / AC and SPI = EV / PV.

Open for the E-06-04 / E-04-03 breakdown:
- **Verification map:** which ITP lines verify which progress steps (rules of credit / weighted steps per progress_unit level, asset-and-tagging.md `earned_qty`). Planner default: each rule-of-credit step names the ITP line(s) that verify it; a step with no ITP line earns on the supervisor's progress sign-off (PER-03) instead, flagged "not QA-verified".
- **Partial credit:** whether a passed line verifies a partial quantity (for example 40 m of 100 m coated). Planner default: yes, the verified quantity is the quantity on the passed ITR.
- **Reversal:** an ITR later voided and reissued (TD-514) or a `RE_INSPECT` withdraws the credit until passed again.
- Claims stay gated as today (commercial-and-claims.md §Upstream gates); this only changes what EV counts.
