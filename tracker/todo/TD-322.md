T-0054 implements the client secret (KERP_ENTRA_CLIENT_SECRET from the secret store). Diff §4.5 allows 'the client secret or certificate'. A certificate credential (private_key_jwt client assertion, key from the secret store) avoids secret expiry/rotation every 6-24 months. Add KERP_ENTRA_CLIENT_CERTIFICATE(_FILE) + thumbprint and sign a client assertion in HttpEntraTransport.redeem_code; keep the secret as fallback.
