The user's T-0184 (2026-11-21) extends shelf life of material batches (coatings, epoxies, electrodes). No spec section or task defines a material batch/lot with a use-by date: materials-and-traceability.md has heat/MTR trace (T-0205, Phase 2, welding) and MRIR receiving (E-03-03, Phase 3); stock lots are site_inventory (E-03-04, Phase 3). Planner default (not blocking now, applied when E-03-03 is broken down): the materials module owns the batch record (batch number, heat number when metallic, material class, original_use_by_date immutable once set, effective_use_by_date changed only by an approved extension through the materials service), site_inventory references it by id. T-0184 and T-0268 are therefore Phase 3 and depend on E-03-03. Question: confirm the owner and that use-by tracking is a Phase 3 feature (not Phase 1 as pasted).
