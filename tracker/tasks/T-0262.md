## Goal
The two-lane QA window (quality-and-inspection.md §QA verification queue and two-lane workbench).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] QA Verification Inbox (RegisterPage over T-0181) and a full-height drawer opened from the inbox or a notification deep link
- [ ] Top bar: Accept/Verify, Reject (mandatory non-blank reason, optional rectification follow-up), Reassign/Forward (user or persona + note); buttons hidden/disabled without QA authority
- [ ] Left lane: record details side by side with OCR values, discrepancies highlighted; a history selector of superseded certs/MTRs/documents switches the right lane
- [ ] Right lane: PDF/image viewer over presigned URLs (T-0016) with zoom and page controls; PDF rendering through a permissively licensed library loaded lazily (planner default pdf.js, Apache-2.0)
- [ ] e2e: a non-QA upload creates a notification whose deep link opens the window; QA toggles to a historical cert, approves (activate + supersede), rejects with a reason, forwards
- [ ] All strings through `t()`; keyboard accessible
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
