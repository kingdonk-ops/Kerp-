## Goal
A password change must invalidate every token issued at or before it, including one issued
earlier in the same second (`iat` has whole-second precision) (repo review 2026-11-13,
finding 4). Also delete the unused `require_tenant_id` / `require_project_id` helpers
(finding 6, tenancy part).
