## Goal
Accept queued offline formal sign-offs at sync without losing evidence or releasing anything unduly (quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation, *Offline branch at sync*). Split out of T-0289 (PR #143 review round 1).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `attest_offline(context, kind, record, intent, offline_evidence)` in `signing/service.py`, called by the consumers' sync handlers: `reauth_method = device_pin_offline` needs **no** server signing grant; the mutation carries `reauth_at` with time evidence (diff §6.4.1), the device id and the `intent_version` the device showed; `ip_source = sync`
- [ ] Device check: accepted only if the device is active, enrolled to the signer and not revoked before `occurred_at` (T-0143); otherwise refused like any other mutation from that device
- [ ] Past the device check nothing is dropped and nothing answers 409: an `intent_version` that was current at `occurred_at` (T-0289 registry history) is accepted as is; one that was not is kept, effective, rendered with the text of the version shown, and flagged `intent_outdated`
- [ ] Signer standing via the identity service (no table access): if the signer was `is_active = false`, or had their sessions revoked (T-0244) after the device session the mutation was queued under began, at any time before `occurred_at`, the sign-off is kept in the ledger as evidence but is **not effective** (not counted as signed, no hold release, no row closed), flagged `signer_inactive` and routed to the QA verification queue (T-0181) through the qms service; a later reactivation does not make it effective
- [ ] Outside the offline grace/window of diff §4.2 it is flagged `grace_exceeded` (kept, effective, as T-0177)
- [ ] Flags are written to `attestation.flags` and the audit payload; `intent_outdated` and `grace_exceeded` items are listed for QA review
- [ ] Tests: offline sign-off across an intent-version bump (current at `occurred_at` → accepted unflagged; older → `intent_outdated`, effective); offline sign-off made after a deactivation of the signer the way SCIM does it (`is_active = false` + T-0244 revoke) → kept, `signer_inactive`, hold not released, QA item created, still not effective after reactivation and outbox drain; offline sign-off after a force sign-out → same; mutation from a revoked device → refused; grace exceeded → flagged, effective; no grant required; attestation verified by `verify_chain`
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
