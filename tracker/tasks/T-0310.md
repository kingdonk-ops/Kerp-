## Goal
Accept queued offline formal sign-offs at sync without losing evidence or releasing anything unduly (quality-and-inspection.md §Signing attestation, *Offline branch at sync*). Split out of T-0289 (PR #143 review round 1); signer standing made durable and QA routing defined in review round 2. Owns the identity-service standing record and query that T-0299 (SCIM) reuses.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `attest_offline(context, kind, record, intent, offline_evidence)` in `signing/service.py`, called by the consumers' sync handlers: `reauth_method = device_pin_offline` needs **no** server signing grant; the mutation carries `reauth_at` with time evidence (diff §6.4.1, T-0177 `time_confidence`), the device id and the `intent_version` the device showed; `ip_source = sync`
- [ ] Device check: accepted only if the device is active, enrolled to the signer and not revoked before `occurred_at` (T-0143); otherwise refused like any other mutation from that device
- [ ] Past the device check nothing is dropped and nothing answers 409: an `intent_version` that was current at `occurred_at` (T-0289 registry history) is accepted as is; one that was not is kept, effective and flagged `intent_outdated`. The device sends only the `intent_version` it showed; the server renders that version's i18n text in the signer's locale and never accepts client-supplied intent text (test: a client-supplied `intent_text` is ignored)
- [ ] Durable standing record (identity module, additive): new table `oe_identity_account_standing_event` (`BaseRecordMixin` + tenant RLS; `user_id`, `event` `deactivated | reactivated | sessions_revoked | signed_in`, `occurred_at`, `device_id` for `signed_in`, `source` `admin | force_signout | scim | sign_in`; append-only, soft delete only; index `(tenant_id, user_id, occurred_at)`). A new identity-module table, not a column on `auth_user`, so no core-table ADR is needed. `record_standing_event(...)` in `identity/service.py` is called by the admin deactivate/reactivate path (T-0014, through the service), force sign-out (T-0244) and every successful sign-in (password, SSO, magic link, device enrolment); T-0299 (SCIM) calls it for its deactivate/reactivate
- [ ] **Signer standing:** `signer_standing(user_id, from_ts, to_ts)` in the identity service answers from durable standing events persisted in the identity table `oe_identity_account_standing_event` (`deactivated | reactivated | sessions_revoked | signed_in`, with `occurred_at` and, for `signed_in`, the `device_id`), written by admin deactivation and reactivation (T-0014 path), force sign-out (T-0244), SCIM (T-0299) and every successful sign-in; it never reads the expiring Redis session index. The window starts at the signer's last successful sign-in on the mutation's `device_id` at or before `occurred_at` (no such record: at the signer's earliest recorded event, fail-safe) and ends at `occurred_at`; when `time_confidence` is neither `MONOTONIC_RECONSTRUCTED` nor `VERIFIED_ONLINE`, it ends at the sync time instead (fail-safe against a backdated clock). If a deactivation or a session revocation of the signer falls inside the window, the sign-off is **kept as evidence but not effective**: it does not count as signed, releases no hold and closes no row, and it is flagged `signer_inactive`. A deactivation and reactivation both before the window start do not flag it; a later reactivation never makes a flagged sign-off effective.
- [ ] `signer_standing(user_id, from_ts, to_ts)` in `identity/service.py` returns whether a `deactivated` or `sessions_revoked` event falls in the window plus the matching events; signing calls it through the service only (no table access)
- [ ] **QA routing:** a `signer_inactive` sign-off creates a QA verification item of target kind `offline_signoff_signer_inactive`, whose provider is registered in `signing/service.py`. Neither approve nor reject ever makes the original sign-off effective: the provider's `apply_approved` records the flag as reviewed and `apply_rejected` records the entry void, each only as a ledger append; the flagged entry is never altered and nothing is released or closed. The point is resolved only by a fresh attested online sign-off by a qualified signer through the normal consumer path, or by recording the flagged entry void.
- [ ] Outside the offline grace/window of diff §4.2 it is flagged `grace_exceeded` (kept, effective, as T-0177)
- [ ] Flags are written to `attestation.flags` and the audit payload; `intent_outdated` and `grace_exceeded` items are listed for QA review
- [ ] Tests:
  - offline sign-off across an intent-version bump (current at `occurred_at` → accepted unflagged; older → `intent_outdated`, effective, server-rendered text of the shown version)
  - deactivate + force sign-out of the signer after the device sign-in, fake clock past 12 h + offline grace so the Redis session entries have expired, reactivate, drain the outbox → still `signer_inactive`, hold not released, QA item created
  - offline sign-off after a force sign-out alone → `signer_inactive`
  - a user deactivated and reactivated **before** the device sign-in that began the session → not flagged, effective
  - low-trust time: `time_confidence = SKEW_ADJUSTED` or `UNVERIFIED_REBOOT`, `occurred_at` backdated before a deactivation that happened before sync → `signer_inactive`; the same with `MONOTONIC_RECONSTRUCTED` and the deactivation after `occurred_at` → not flagged
  - approving or rejecting the `offline_signoff_signer_inactive` QA item never releases the point and never alters the flagged ledger entry (`verify_chain` still passes); a fresh online attested sign-off by a qualified signer releases it
  - mutation from a revoked device → refused; grace exceeded → flagged, effective; no grant required
