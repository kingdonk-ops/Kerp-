## Goal
Flag a resource that expires before the work or inspection that needs it (quality-and-inspection.md §Schedule readiness conflicts). Part 2 of the user's T-0183; P6 activity targets T-0267, work-plan lines with E-04-12.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `oe_schedule_readiness_conflict` (`BaseRecordMixin` + project RLS): `resource_kind` (`equipment_calibration | staff_credential | material_use_by`), `resource_id`, `resource_label`, `expiry_date`, `target_kind` (`p6_activity | work_plan_line | scheduled_inspection`), `target_id`, `target_label`, `target_planned_start`, `target_planned_finish`, `anchor_node_id`, `status` (`open | acknowledged | resolved`), `last_notified_at`, `resolved_at`; unique `(project_id, resource_kind, resource_id, target_kind, target_id)`; index `(project_id, status, expiry_date)`
- [ ] Target-provider seam (targets with their required resources): inspections (T-0152 planned start/due date, assignee's required credentials, instrument references on the template) here; P6 (T-0267) and work plans (E-04-12) register later; resources come from the T-0183 providers; no cross-module joins
- [ ] Conflict when a required resource's expiry date < the target's planned finish (or the inspection date); upsert on the unique key; no longer true → `resolved` automatically
- [ ] Evaluated by the daily cron and reactively on SAQ events: inspection date/assignee change (T-0152), equipment or credential expiry change, and a verification approved through T-0181 (renewal) — the conflict resolves and the badge data clears (test)

- [ ] `GET /api/v1/compliance/readiness-conflicts?status=&target_kind=&target_id=&resource_kind=&resource_id=` and `POST /{id}/acknowledge` (PER-03/05/06); site scope (PER-07) applied; another project → 404
