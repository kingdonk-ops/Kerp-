## Goal
TD-212 (screens): the inspector records short video clips and audio notes on an answer, offline.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Video capture records MP4 within the limits (stops at 60 s), makes a JPEG poster on the device and stores clip and poster through `MediaStore`; audio capture records M4A and stops at 5 min
- [ ] Over-limit or unsupported recordings are refused on the device with a message; clip and note counts per answer enforced
- [ ] Playback works offline from local media and online from presigned URLs
- [ ] Storage usage shown against the device media budget (diff §6.2)
- [ ] 48 dp targets; all strings through `t()`; component tests with a fake recorder
- [ ] `frontend/README.md` updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
