## Goal
TD-214 item 1: the field screen where the inspector does the inspection offline, plus starting an ad-hoc inspection.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A downloaded inspection opens in the Dynamic Form Renderer with its pinned template revision, CEL visibility/required/range rules and localised strings (T-0047), entirely offline
- [ ] Every answer change is written through the storage contract and the outbox at once (autosave per field); killing the app and reopening resumes with no answer lost (test)
- [ ] The instrument picker reuses the calibration-gated tool pick of T-0130; expired tools are not selectable
- [ ] Answer conflict flags (T-0148) and return comments (T-0151) are shown on their answers
- [ ] Sign and submit: proposed result pass/fail, local PIN signature, geolocation when allowed; submit disabled while uploads are pending or a conflict flag is open
- [ ] Ad-hoc: on a downloaded Item or `inspection_anchor` node the inspector picks a template allowed for that anchor and creates an inspection with a client-generated UUIDv7 id, offline
- [ ] 48 dp targets, high-contrast tokens; all strings through `t()`
- [ ] `frontend/README.md` updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
