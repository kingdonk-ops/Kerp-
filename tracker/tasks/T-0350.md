## Goal
Record per external party whether the RFI call-out actually reached anyone outside the app, and treat an undelivered call-out like short notice for that party (quality-and-inspection.md §`inspection_requests` §2, §5, §6, §7, §9 "Delivery failure per party"; product intake 2026-11-21, planner default). Split out of T-0335 so T-0335 stays S (PR #177 review round 1).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Additive migration: `notice_delivery` (`pending | delivered | failed`, default `pending`), nullable `notice_delivery_reason` (`bounced | suppressed | no_channel | provider_error | skipped`) and nullable boolean `notice_valid_at_outcome` on `oe_inspection_requests_slot_party`; RLS unchanged
- [ ] Delivery judged over push and e-mail only (in-app always exists): a party becomes `failed` when every push and e-mail attempt for all its call-out recipients of the latest issue or reschedule failed or was skipped, `delivered` once any attempt succeeds; results read from the persisted per-channel delivery log (TD-238) and the core-mail delivery ledger (T-0226, `recipient_user_id`/`project_id`) through their services, never their tables; a reschedule resets the party to `pending` (tests: bounced e-mail + no device → `failed`/`bounced`; suppressed address → `failed`/`suppressed`; one delivered push → `delivered`)
- [ ] On `failed`: a `high` in-app notification to the requester and PER-06 (once per party per issue/reschedule) and an `inspection_requests` event; attendance is never set by delivery (test)
- [ ] Valid notice per party = `notice_met` and `notice_delivery != failed`, evaluated at the outcome; the witness check (`witness_status`, T-0333) and the no-show delay rule (T-0334) use it: a `W` party absent after a failed delivery leaves the requirement unmet and creates a delay entry with cause `contractor` (tests for W and H)
- [ ] Validity is frozen at the outcome: recording `witnessed`, `no_show`, `failed` (or the slot-level `waived`) stores `slot_party.notice_valid_at_outcome` (boolean, null before; additive column); `witness_status` and delay creation read only this value; a later delivery result updates `notice_delivery`, emits an event and is shown, but never changes the outcome's effect; a reschedule clears it with the outcome (test: `no_show` with the W party's delivery `pending`, then a bounce arrives → `witness_status` and the delay table unchanged, `notice_delivery = failed` and an event recorded)
- [ ] A recipient's own channel opt-out counts as `skipped` for that attempt (planner default, TD-549)
- [ ] API: per-party `notice_delivery` and reason on the request detail and calendar responses (PER-15 sees them; no `internal_commercial` fields)
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
