## Goal
Close TD-298 (review finding 5). `POST /api/v1/devices/{device_id}/wipe-confirmed` is unauthenticated and audits every refusal, including unknown ids, so anyone can grow the audit ledger without limit. Owner decision 2026-11-15: keep auditing every refusal (T-0080) and add a per-client progressive lockout before the device lookup.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A per-client (`request.client.host`) `ProgressiveLockout` runs before the device lookup; once locked the endpoint answers 429 with `Retry-After` and writes no audit entry
- [x] The lockout is keyed per client and escalates and decays like the login lockout (free attempts, doubling delay, window); a successful wipe confirmation is never blocked by another client's failures
- [x] `WipeConfirmRequest.wipe_secret` has `max_length=128` (422 whatever the device)
- [x] Tests: N refusals from one client then 429 and no new audit rows; another client unaffected; over-long secret 422
- [ ] All quality gates green

## Notes
Behind a load balancer the per-client key depends on uvicorn's trusted-proxy setting (TD-138); with every client seen as the proxy, a flood only delays real wipe confirmations (the app retries).

## Context
Repo review 2026-11-15, owner-accepted (finding 5, security: low). Supersedes TD-298.

## Notes
- 2026-11-16: `WIPE_CONFIRM_POLICY` (lockout.py): 10 free failures, 30 s doubling to 15 min, 1 h window, keyed by `request.client.host`; a right secret clears the client's counter; the store being down answers 503 like login. The `429` is documented in OpenAPI (generated client regenerated).
