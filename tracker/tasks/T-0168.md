## Goal
TD-212 (reference images): a question can show up to three reference images to the inspector, offline.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A template question may list up to 3 reference images (JPEG/PNG, ≤ 5 MB each) with optional localised captions; more or larger → 422 (planner defaults)
- [ ] Images are uploaded through T-0016 presigned uploads; their sha256 is part of the template revision's canonical hash; changing an image makes a new revision (freeze test)
- [ ] Template revisions in a device's pull scope bring their reference images (exposed through the `forms` service for T-0147)
- [ ] `forms` `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
