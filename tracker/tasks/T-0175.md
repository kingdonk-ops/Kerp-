## Goal
Client side of co-sign mode (ii) (user, 2026-11-13) in the KERP Android app and PWA, signed in as PER-15.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] On first use the app generates an ES256 key pair (Android Keystore, or a non-extractable WebCrypto key in the PWA) and enrols the public key (T-0173); the private key is never exported (test)
- [ ] "Co-sign" scans the inspector's challenge QR (T-0155 scanner adapter); if the record is on the device, the app checks the hash against its own copy and shows its copy, otherwise it shows the challenge summary and says to read the record on the tablet
- [ ] Approve signs the assertion (canonical JSON + ES256 with §6.4.1 time evidence from T-0172) and shows it as one QR of at most 1,200 bytes (compressed, base45; planner default); works offline after local unlock
- [ ] A hash mismatch or an expired local session blocks signing with a clear message
- [ ] Uses the shared assertion fixtures of T-0163
- [ ] 48 dp targets; all strings through `t()`; tests with fake keystore and fake scanner
- [ ] `frontend/README.md` updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
