## Epic
Field findings that the scoped work is wrong (already done, not found, worse than scoped,
steel removed) become change-of-scope records anchored to the work anchor, with a snapshot of
the current scope, proposed lines, photos, optional joint walk-down and a thread of client /
contractor responses. Agreed changes are applied as a new revision of the anchor's scope
fields; open plans including the anchor are flagged; commercial impact links to a variation
(E-06-03). Reference: the change-of-scope tracker (Site | Asset | RSW | Current scope |
Comment | Client response | Contractor response). Detail in the spec §6.
