## Goal
QA gets a structured first draft of a project ITP from the master template, scope and requirements, refines it with targeted AI rewrites, and publishes it as a frozen revision only by a human decision (quality-and-inspection.md §ITP authoring, assignment and propagation *AI-assisted authoring*).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AI governance (CLAUDE.md §6): every AI call runs as a SAQ job (heavy client libraries imported lazily); the provider is the deployment's configured AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or a self-hosted Hugging Face model, hosted in an Australian region, TD-078; read-only deployment configuration, diff §4.7 / TD-398; `none` → 409 `AI_PROVIDER_NOT_CONFIGURED`); AI output is stored with `source="ai"` as a proposal and changes nothing until a human confirms; prompts and responses are never logged in full (hash + token counts only); synthetic fixtures and a fake provider in tests
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/qms/itp-templates/ai-draft` `{master_template_key, scope_node_id, requirement_ids[], instructions?}` (PER-06 on the project; PER-13 for tenant masters): enqueues an `interactive` SAQ job; inputs read through the owning services only: the master ITP revision (T-0113), the scope subtree and level definitions (T-0101; tasks once E-04-07 exists), project requirements and specifications (T-0120), the published ITR form templates (T-0110)
- [ ] Output validated against the ITP revision schema (TD-428 default: ordered stages → lines with activity, acceptance criteria, spec/code references, ITR template key + revision, intervention point per party Contractor / Principal / Client from `H`/`W`/`R`/`S`/`M`); invalid output or an unknown ITR template → job fails with a reason, nothing saved; valid output saved as a **draft revision** with `source="ai"` and a provenance block (model, input refs, hashes)
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/qms/itp-templates/{key}/revisions/{rev}/ai-rewrite` `{line_ids | stage_ids, instructions}` on a draft only: job returns a proposed diff; nothing changes until `POST .../ai-rewrite/{job}/accept` (all or selected hunks) by the user; manual edits on the draft as usual
- [ ] Publish (freeze) of a revision whose draft has AI content: PER-06 (project) / PER-13 (tenant master) only; never the job, PER-09 or `system_admin` alone (ADR 0016); ledger entry (T-0006) records the publisher and that AI content was confirmed
- [ ] An unpublished (draft) revision cannot be instantiated: `run_checks` rule `itp.template_published` (T-0188) blocks it (test with an AI draft)
- [ ] Tests with a fake provider: valid draft; schema-invalid output rejected; rewrite diff accepted partially; publish by PER-09 → 403
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
