## Goal
TD-169 (per inspection): the reviewer gets a PDF of an inspection (quality-and-inspection.md §Inspection report).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/inspections/{id}/report` enqueues a `batch` SAQ job and returns the job id; the job stores the PDF through the storage service with its sha256 and returns a presigned link
- [ ] Reports exist for `SUBMITTED` (watermarked "Not approved") and approved records; other states → 409; an unchanged record version reuses the stored report
- [ ] On approval the report is rendered and its object key and sha256 are kept on the record
- [ ] Content as specified: header (project, anchor path in project terminology, Item tag, template name and revision, RFI no. when linked, inspection date, entered time, backdating flag and reason), answers in template order with labels and units, conflict flags, photos with annotations burned in plus capture time and location, linked NCR/punch references, inspector and reviewer signatures (name, persona, UTC and site-local time, geolocation, ledger content hash), review rounds and return reasons, footer (record id, page x of y, generation time)
- [ ] Fixed labels (headings, "Not approved" watermark, "page x of y") come from a backend message catalogue keyed like the frontend `t()` keys, rendered in the template's `default_language` with `en` fallback; no hard-coded user-visible label in the renderer (test switches the catalogue) (planner default)
- [ ] `signed_at`, `received_at` and `clock_suspect` of the submit signature are printed
- [ ] PyMuPDF is imported lazily inside the job (T-0022 lint passes); a test renders a synthetic inspection, asserts the text content and that an annotated photo differs from its original
- [ ] Tenant and project come from the job context; another project's inspection → 404
- [ ] `inspections` `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
