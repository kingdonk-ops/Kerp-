## Goal
Inspector side of the three co-sign modes (user R1a; co-sign redesign 2026-11-13).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] "Hand to client" locks the inspector's work and opens a co-sign screen showing only the inspection being signed, read-only; no navigation, other records or the inspector's session are reachable (test)
- [ ] Mode i (online): client username/e-mail + signing PIN → co-sign token (T-0162); lockout messages from the server shown
- [ ] Mode ii (QR): the tablet shows the challenge QR (inspection id, version, record hash, step, summary, fresh 128-bit nonce), scans the client's assertion QR and verifies it offline against the downloaded public keys (signature, nonce, record hash, signer scope); a failed check is shown with its reason and nothing is queued; uses the shared fixtures of T-0163
- [ ] Mode iii (wet): the client draws a signature and types their name, the inspector picks the PER-15 account; queued as provisional with a visible "provisional" label
- [ ] No client PIN hash or other client secret is stored on the tablet (test inspects the store)
- [ ] After signing or cancelling, everything the client entered except the co-sign mutation is cleared, and the app returns to the inspector only after the inspector's PIN (T-0143)
- [ ] 48 dp targets, high-contrast tokens; all strings through `t()`; tests with fake network, fake scanner and fake keystore
- [ ] `frontend/README.md` updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
