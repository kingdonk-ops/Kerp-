## Goal
TD-213 (screens): the inspector hands the device to the client, the client signs with their own account and PIN, and the device returns to the inspector only after the inspector's PIN.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] "Hand to client" locks the inspector's work and opens a co-sign screen showing only the inspection being signed, read-only; no navigation, other records or the inspector's session are reachable (test)
- [ ] The client signs in with username or e-mail and signing PIN: online through a co-sign token, offline against the downloaded signer verifier; five wrong PINs block that signer on the device until online
- [ ] The client draws the signature and confirms; the co-sign mutation is queued through the outbox
- [ ] After signing or cancelling, everything the client entered except the signature mutation is cleared, and the app returns to the inspector only after the inspector's PIN (T-0143 local unlock)
- [ ] 48 dp targets, high-contrast tokens; all strings through `t()`; tests with fake network and fake keystore
- [ ] `frontend/README.md` updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
