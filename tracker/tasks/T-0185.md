## Goal
A register of approved product substitutions with traceable authority (materials-and-traceability.md §Product substitutions). Register and internal-QA route of the user's T-0185; baseline list T-0269, CEL helper T-0270, offline/PDF T-0271, tablet selector T-0272, client TQ route T-0273 (Phase 5).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `oe_material_substitution` (`BaseRecordMixin` + project RLS): `ref` (project numbering pattern `{project}-SUB-{seq:4}` through the T-0108 numbering mechanism, unique per project), `approval_route` (`client_tq | internal_qa`), `tq_id`, `tq_ref` (nullable; used by T-0273), `scope_kind` (`project | node_subtree | work_anchor | item`) with `scope_node_id` / `scope_item_id` consistent with the kind (422 otherwise), `original_product_code/name`, `substitute_product_code/name`, `justification` (non-blank), `equivalency_document_id` (complete T-0016 upload), `status` (`draft | pending_approval | approved | rejected | revoked`), `requested_by/at`, `approved_by/at`, `approval_signature_id`, `rejection_reason`; index `(project_id, status, original_product_code, substitute_product_code)`
- [ ] Project setting `substitution_approver_personas` (default PER-06; PER-13 allowed per TD-422); approval needs an equivalency document or a documented engineering rationale (non-blank, ≥ 50 characters, planner default)
- [ ] `internal_qa`: an authorized QE submits and a different authorized QE approves (TD-423 default; `allow_self_approval` = true on the project lets the raiser approve, flagged `self_approved` in the ledger); approval signs through the T-0119 sign-off ledger (`approval_signature_id`) and writes a T-0006 entry
- [ ] A draft by a user without approver authority goes to `pending_approval` and creates a T-0181 verification item with `target_kind = material_substitution` (justification and equivalency document side by side in T-0262); approve/reject through the queue calls this module's provider
- [ ] Reject needs a reason and notifies the requester; revoke (approver persona, reason) stops future use, keeps past bindings and is audited
- [ ] `client_tq` substitutions can be stored as drafts with a `tq_ref` but cannot be approved until T-0273 (422 with a reason)
- [ ] `materials.service.approved_substitutions(project, anchor, as_of)` returns approved substitutions whose scope covers the anchor (subtree through the asset service lineage) and that were approved on or before `as_of` and not revoked by then
- [ ] Another project's record → 404 (test)
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
