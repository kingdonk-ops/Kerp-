## Epic
Individual, multi-file and ZIP uploads; originals immutable (sha256). Split combined PDFs by
text/metadata, page OCR, template recognition (versioned legacy/current layouts), RSW-number and
page-sequence detection; AI only where rules are unsure. Store original, page numbers, split PDFs
and page-to-RSW map. Every extracted value keeps original text, numeric value, unit, source
page/field and confidence. Two passes (local extraction, then AI/vision for low-confidence
fields). Per-document and batch progress, retry, failed-document queue; one failure never fails
the batch. Blocked on TD-078 (processing location).
