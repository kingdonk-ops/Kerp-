## Epic
Planners prepare jobs (e.g. a shutdown) while the P6 programme runs: a work plan either runs
alongside the programme (**overlay**) or stops programme work in its scope from its start day
and lets it resume afterwards (**interrupt**, displaced forecast). Plan lines anchor to the
work anchor (RSW) or any node; resources are allocated by resource group (trade) or named
people/crews per day and shift, checked against roster capacity and credentials. Issued
revisions go to client review (E-07-04); on approval the plan is committed into the KERP layer
of the programme (tasks, bookings, P6 fragnet export) and shown overlaid on the schedule
(Gantt, histogram, calendar, kanban). Reference: P6 XER (shut calendars, Shutdown/Online
activity codes, trade resources), basis of schedule, one-week shut resource allocation sheet,
front-end planning workbook. Full detail and suggested breakdown in the spec §14.

Open decisions / gaps: TD-080 (ADR 0006), TD-083 (resource pool vs HR cull), TD-100 (shared role/trade catalogue). At breakdown: resource-pool tasks carry `blocked_by: [TD-083]`; programme-layer / commit tasks carry `blocked_by: [TD-080]` until ADR 0006 is accepted.

**Status `needs-breakdown`:** the planner agent splits this into S/M tasks (`T-xxxx`, `epic: E-04-12`) when its phase is next. Do not implement an epic directly.
