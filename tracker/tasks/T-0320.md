## Goal
The **Issues** menu item is where issue templates (issue types: punch or NCR per category) are created and managed (user, 2026-11-21; issues.md §2; diff §6.1), separate from the **Templates** menu of inspection templates (T-0328). Both open the shared designer (E-01-03) for fields. For the project's QA authority, PER-09, and PER-13 on the tenant library.
