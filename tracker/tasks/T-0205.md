## Goal
Link pipe/fitting **heat numbers** and **MTR (mill cert)** properties to each weld joint. MTR OCR
runs in the **SAQ** `interactive` queue (not ARQ) and writes chemistry/mechanicals into weld joint
properties in `wps`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Each weld joint links the pipe/fitting `heat_number`(s) and the source MTR document (uploads, T-0016)
- [ ] MTR OCR runs as a **SAQ `interactive`** job (T-0015), with heavy OCR libs imported lazily; never on the request path and never ARQ
- [ ] OCR extracts chemistry/mechanicals (yield, tensile, carbon equivalent %CE) and writes them to the weld joint properties consumed by `wps`
- [ ] Heat → MTR → weld trace is queryable mill-to-weld (heat numbers resolve to every weld execution id)
- [ ] Each weld joint side records `steel_origin` (`new`/`existing`) and, for new steel, `supplied_by` (`kaefer`/`client`) and `heat_number`; an `existing` side needs no heat number and appears as "existing steel" in the MDR heat trace (welding-and-nde.md §Heat / MTR traceability, TD-091)
- [ ] A `new` + `kaefer` side without a heat number raises an NCR through the `ncr` service when the weld is submitted (test) (TD-091)
- [ ] A `new` + `client` side without a heat number hard-locks the weld: its inspection fails the `check` gate until a heat number is entered or a signed client-rep waiver record (signer, date, reason, scope) is attached to the inspection; the lock lifts in both cases (tests) (TD-091)
- [ ] The waiver record is included in the MDR heat trace (TD-091)
- [ ] Trace tables use `BaseRecordMixin` and have a project-scoped RLS policy; cross-module access to `wps` goes through its `service.py` (no cross-module table access)
- [ ] `materials` module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
