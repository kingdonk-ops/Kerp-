## Epic
One template designer used by both inspection templates and issue templates (user, 2026-11-21; one-engine, no per-module field designers, CLAUDE.md §6 / quality-and-inspection.md §One field-type registry). After Pilot-0: until then templates come from client packs, seeds (T-0322) and the API.

Scope:
- sections (add, remove, reorder), fields from `core/fields`, required / optional, help text and localised labels (edge §2.2), CEL `visible_if` / `required_if` / `calculated` / range validation with live compile, reference images;
- preview through the Dynamic Form Renderer;
- draft → publish as a new revision (records keep the revision they pinned); permissions per layer (tenant library PER-13; project QA authority / PER-09);
- inspection templates: category / standalone flag, header role bindings, post-submit rules (reuses T-0258), `issue_category`;
- issue templates: type, category, `default_origin`, flag defaults (`visibility`, `mdr_effect`, `completion_effect`), workflow step per section (issues.md §2).
