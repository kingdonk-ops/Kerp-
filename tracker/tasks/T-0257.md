- [ ] `oe_inspections_follow_up` (`BaseRecordMixin` + RLS): `inspection_id`, `anchor_node_id`, `anchor_item_id` (exactly one; T-0288 adds `equipment_id` additively), `kind` (`tagout_confirmation | repair_request | supervisor_review`), `title`, `status` (`open | tagout_confirmed | resolved | cancelled`), `blocks_return_to_service`, `assigned_persona`, `assigned_user_id`, `due_at` (SLA hours from the config), `tagout_confirmed_by/at/notes`, `tagout_photo_attachment_id`, `resolved_by/at`, `resolution_notes`

- [ ] The assigned supervisor (PER-04/PER-03) gets a pinned high-priority notification 'Confirm physical tagout applied' (pin cleared by confirmation through `clear_pins`)

- [ ] A failing node/Item inspection with a `tagout_confirmation` rule: blocking follow-up open, supervisor notified, tagout follow-up created (integration test, end to end without equipment)
- [ ] Every step is an action-log row and a ledger entry (`source = "automation"` for automatic steps, the user's for confirmations)
