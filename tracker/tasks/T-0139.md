## Goal
The stateless `eligibility` seam (split out of T-0111): "is this person / instrument / material
valid on the inspection date?", reading registers only through their owning modules' services.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `eligibility` module owns no tables; one entry point (e.g. `evaluate(subject, requirement, as_of) -> EligibilityResult(passed, reason, source_ref)`) whose signature is hash-checked at M1 (T-0131) (quality-and-inspection.md §eligibility)
- [ ] Validity is evaluated **as of** the given date (the inspection date): a credential valid on that date passes even if expired today; one expired on that date fails (ADR 0004 Amendment C)
- [ ] A missing expiry on a type that expires fails closed with a reason
- [ ] Registers plug in through a provider seam registered by the owning module's `service.py` (credentials T-0118 now; calibration T-0112, WPS and materials later); no cross-module table access (CLAUDE.md §6)
- [ ] Result reasons are user-readable and i18n-keyed (e.g. "Torque wrench TW-12: calibration expired on <date>")
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
