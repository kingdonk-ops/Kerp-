## Goal
ADR 0003 Amendment 3: restructure the tree after work exists without rewriting history. Records
stay on the node they were captured on and roll up through lineage.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `POST /assets/nodes/{id}/split` retires the source, creates the new nodes with `derived_from = [source]`, and sets the source's `superseded_by` to them (asset-and-tagging.md §Restructure with history)
- [ ] `POST /assets/nodes/merge` retires the sources, sets their `superseded_by` to the target, and the target's `derived_from` to the sources
- [ ] `POST /assets/nodes/{id}/retire` sets `status = retired`; the service refuses new anchors (work or inspection) on a retired node
- [ ] Every operation re-checks the anchor invariants (T-0101) and never changes a level key; each is audit-ledgered
- [ ] `service.lineage_ids(node_ids)` returns the transitive predecessors; records anchored to a predecessor are included in a successor's contextual queries without being re-pointed (test with split then merge)
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
