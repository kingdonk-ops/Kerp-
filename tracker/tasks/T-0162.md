## Goal
TD-213 (server side): a client representative (PER-15) can prove who they are on an inspector's device, online or offline, with their own account and signing PIN.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A PER-15 user sets and resets a 6-digit signing PIN online; only an Argon2id hash with its parameters is stored; the PIN is separate from device PINs (T-0038)
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/signing/cosign-tokens` (username or e-mail + signing PIN + inspection id), called from the inspector's session, returns a single-use co-sign token for that inspection valid 5 minutes; wrong PIN → 401 and counts toward lockout; a signer who is not an active PER-15 member of the project with the anchor in their granted subtree → 403
- [ ] The pull scope (T-0147) delivers a signer verifier (hash + parameters, user id, display name, granted subtree) for each PER-15 member whose subtree covers a picked anchor, and a `remove` delta when the membership or grant ends
- [ ] Five wrong PINs for one signer on one device block co-sign for that signer on that device until an online sign-in; failures reported by the device are recorded per signer and device
- [ ] The signer verifier is never returned to any other endpoint; tests prove another project's or tenant's signers are never sent
- [ ] Identity `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
