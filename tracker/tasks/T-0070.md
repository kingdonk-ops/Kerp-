## Goal
A deployment that forgets `KERP_ENVIRONMENT` must fail safe: production defaults (no
ephemeral JWT key, no in-memory revocation, magic-link tokens never logged). Development,
tests and CI opt in to `development` explicitly (repo review 2026-11-13, needs-validation C;
decided by the owner).
