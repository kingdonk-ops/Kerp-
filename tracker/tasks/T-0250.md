## Goal
Pre-starts, equipment checks and fault reports name the equipment they are about through an informational **equipment link** (user, 2026-11-21; ADR 0003 amendment 2026-11-21; ADR 0014 superseded; TD-411 resolved). This task adds the equipment-specific rules on top of the generic links of T-0325.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The `equipment` link-target provider (T-0325) checks through the equipment service: unknown or other project → 404; decommissioned → 422; out-of-service or quarantined → 422 except on a `repair_fault` record (the T-0112 hard gate)
- [ ] There is no `equipment_id` anchor on `oe_inspections_inspection`; an equipment link never counts for roll-ups, completion or progress (test)
- [ ] `GET /api/v1/equipment/{id}/inspections` through `inspections.service.linked_records("equipment", id)`
- [ ] Post-submit automation (T-0180) receives the record's single equipment link as `equipment` in its context; `update_target_fields` on equipment targets that entry through the equipment service whitelist (test with an instrument entry; vehicles and plant once T-0249 lands, TD-412)
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
