## Goal
Standalone pre-starts, equipment checks and fault reports anchor to an equipment register entry (ADR 0014, proposed).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Nullable `equipment_id` and `location_node_id` on `oe_inspections_inspection` (additive; no FK across modules)
- [ ] Exactly one of `node_id` / `item_id` / `equipment_id`; `equipment_id` only when `is_standalone` and `category` ∈ {`safety_prestart`, `equipment_check`, `repair_fault`}; otherwise 422 (tests per case)
- [ ] Equipment is checked through the equipment service: unknown or other project → 404; decommissioned → 422; out-of-service or quarantined → 422 except `repair_fault` (the T-0112 hard gate)
- [ ] `location_node_id` is informational only: not counted by the node inspection roll-up, completion or progress (test)
- [ ] `GET /api/v1/equipment/{id}/inspections` through the inspections service; post-submit automation (T-0180) receives `equipment` in its context
- [ ] CLAUDE.md §6 anchor sentence updated as ADR 0014 point 5 (in the same PR, once TD-411 is accepted)
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
