## Epic
Handover register for back-to-back / FIFO roles (supervisors, QA/QC, inspectors,
coordinators, PMs). Project settings choose which people or personas must hand over and to
whom; handovers are free-form sections with links to records (auto-link while typing, or
select text → link icon → picker) and hover previews; an activity panel suggests what was
started, completed or sent on (status / scope changes) during the swing, selected via a
per-project rule set with defaults; the incoming person sees it pinned as a high-priority
notification and on their first shift, and acknowledges it. Full detail and a suggested
breakdown in `docs/spec/modules/handover.md` §12.
