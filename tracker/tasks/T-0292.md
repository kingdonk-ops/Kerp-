## Goal
Time-bound, audited acting delegates who receive the items addressed to an absent person (quality-and-inspection.md §Delegation of authority). The narrow version pulled forward from E-06-07; routing and on-behalf sign-off are T-0293, screens T-0294.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] New module `delegations` (manifest, models, router, service, schemas, README). Table `oe_delegations_delegation` (`BaseRecordMixin` + project RLS): `delegator_user_id`, `delegate_user_id`, `kinds` (subset of `hold_point | verification | sla_escalation | tq_approval`), `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `reason`, `created_by_role` (`self | project_admin | system_admin`), `revoked_at`, `revoked_by`, `revoke_reason`
- [ ] Rules (422): delegate ≠ delegator; both live members of the project; `ends_at` > `starts_at`, length ≤ 90 days (TD-434); at most one active delegation per delegator, project and kind (partial unique index over live, unrevoked rows)
- [ ] `POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/delegations` by the delegator, or for an absent member by a PER-09 of the project or a `system_admin` (delegator notified); `GET` (own as delegator or delegate; PER-09 and `system_admin` all of the project); `POST /{id}/revoke` (delegator, creator, PER-09, `system_admin`); another project → 404
- [ ] Creation returns a warning (saved anyway) when the delegate does not currently hold a persona that can act on the chosen kinds; the binding check is at the action (T-0293)
- [ ] Service: `active_delegates(project_id, user_id, kind, at)` and `acting_for(project_id, actor_id, assignee_id, kind, at)`; never transitive (a delegate's own delegations are ignored; test)
- [ ] Every create, revoke and expiry audited in `delegations:<project>` (delegator, delegate, kinds, window, actor, role)
- [ ] Delegation never grants a persona, capability, site scope or credential: a unit test asserts `load_grants` is unchanged by an active delegation
- [ ] Module `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
