## Goal
NCR with engineering dispositions (scrap/rework/repair/use-as-is), anchored to any node or Item with the anchor's role recorded (ADR 0003 §2). An inspection `INSPECTED_FAILED` can auto-create an NCR (see T-0111 lifecycle). Client-asset findings (CUI, water, cladding) are not NCRs (TD-106; findings register in TD-117).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] NCR anchors to exactly one node or Item (never a BOQ anchor) and stores `anchor_role` (the role of the anchor's level, e.g. `work_anchor`, `inspection_anchor`, `physical`, or `item`) (ADR 0003 §2)
- [ ] Raising an NCR resolves the work anchor via `asset_management.service.work_anchor_for` (T-0101); an anchor whose path has no work-anchor node → 422 until one is picked or created (TD-113; asset-and-tagging.md §Anchor invariants)
- [ ] Disposition workflow (scrap/rework/repair/use-as-is) with re-inspection close-out
- [ ] The NCR service exposes open NCRs for a work-anchor node, its descendants, its items and its lineage predecessors, so the completion block sits on the work-anchor level (gate lives in T-0124) (asset-and-tagging.md §Anchor invariants)
- [ ] Verifier ≠ resolver enforced in `service.py`
- [ ] Table uses `BaseRecordMixin` and has a project-scoped RLS policy
- [ ] `ncr` `README.md` and docs updated
- [ ] All quality gates green (see CLAUDE.md §Quality gates)
